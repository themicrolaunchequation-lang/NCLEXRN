Questions

Good to know

Who is this for?

Anyone starting out with Lean Six Sigma: students, nurses and healthcare staff, team leaders, and people preparing for a Yellow or Green Belt. Trainers use it as a warm-up or a practice session after teaching a tool.

Do I need to know statistics?

No. Lean Town needs none. Sigma Arcade explains each idea on a card before you play, such as what a p-value or Cpk means, and the game does the maths for you.

Where is my progress saved?

Only in your own browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data resets your stars.

Is this a certification?

No. It's practice that helps the ideas stick. It pairs well with a formal course and exam.

Which world should I start with?

New to Lean Six Sigma? Start in Lean Town, then move on to the Sigma Arcade. Already know the basics? Jump into any arcade cabinet from the DMAIC journey above.
