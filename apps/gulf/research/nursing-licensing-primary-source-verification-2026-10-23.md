## Conflicts, chronology and resolution

- **Qatar duration/count:** Q3 is explicitly dated **24 November 2020**, effective **25 November 2020** for RGN. It is the older dated instrument. Q1/Q5 are live on the read date but provide no RN change-effective date; Q2/Q4/Q7 also lack reliable publication dates. **Which undated publication is newer cannot be established officially.** Q4 FAQs 47–49 defer to the qualifying-exam page, but Q1 still links the contradictory Q2. No dated superseding RN timing instrument was found. Resolver: **DHP Registration & Licensing, DHPRegistration@moph.gov.qa; +974 44092033** (Q6, hotline Wednesdays 08:00–11:00). Prometric can confirm a booked slot, but DHP must reconcile its publications.
- **Qatar attempts:** Q1 and the now-readable **Q8 v04** support five; Q7 is explicitly a backup and places three under physician specialties. The backup suffix `09082023` is not a verified publication date. Treat the RN applicability of three as a question, not an established competing RN rule. Q8's effective/revision date boxes are blank. Ask DHP to confirm the controlling version, exceptions, rounding and reset policy. **This corrects the uploaded pack's statement that Q8 could not be read.**
- **DHA attempts:** D2 is dated **18 July 2025**; D3 is effective **8 May 2025**; U1 is **April 2025**. D2 is newer than those dated documents. D4 is undated, so its chronological position is unknown. D2/D3 support conditional routes absent from D4. Resolver: **info@dha.gov.ae** (D3 §7.2.17), with the exact title and attempt history. Do not promise a fourth attempt without approval.
- **DHA reporting/practice gap:** D3's two-working-day CBT reporting differs from D4's instantaneous-results wording. D2's two-year gap definition differs from U1's three-year general discontinuity threshold; D2 is the newer dated publication. This does not establish that every general PQR rule has been superseded. DHA must identify the controlling RN provisions and effective dates. Five-year same-title recognition and one-year registration validity describe different things and are not, by themselves, contradictory.
- **DOH disclosure/current policy:** A1 **April 2024** calls the cut score internal; no public RN weight table or numerical standard was found in A1, official searches or A2/A3. A2 was updated **5 August 2026** but supplies no replacement blueprint/threshold. U1 **April 2025** is newer than A1 and adds general conditional attempt provisions; ask DOH how they apply. Resolver: **exam@doh.gov.ae; +971 2 666 4442 / UAE 800555** (A2). The finding is an unpublished-in-examined-sources result, not a claim that DOH has confirmed all such information confidential.
- **SCFHS guide/page:** S2 displays **23 October 2026**; S1 has no explicit policy date. S2 has the newer displayed webpage date, but a conclusive supersession comparison is impossible without dating S1. Resolver: **SCFHS 920019393**, or written enquiry through [Tawasul](https://scfhs.org.sa/en/tawasul) (S3). Record this discrepancy without changing SNLE application/source files.
