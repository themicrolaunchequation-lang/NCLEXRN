### Release precautions
1. Do not infer a shared UAE exam blueprint simply because UAE authorities use a unified PQR. Professional *eligibility* and testing specifications are distinct.
2. Do not treat the Qatar older 3½-hour PDF as current timing; the active listing shows 3h. Recheck both before publishing any simulation that claims fidelity.
3. Do not present SCFHS's scaled cutoff 500 as 50%, nor interpret practice accuracy as official pass probability.
4. Do not use historical HAAD/DOH question count/time figures without a current DOH source.
5. Do not imply regulator affiliation, endorsement, certification, or authorization to practice.
6. Actual licensing/eligibility, ID verification, exam booking and fees must be confirmed directly with the authority.
