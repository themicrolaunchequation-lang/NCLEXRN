Subject: Enquiry about the Registered Nurse licensing examination: written confirmation requested

Dear Sir or Madam,

I work with Healthcare Crown Academy, an independent UK-based educational provider. We are preparing original practice material for nurses and want to describe your Registered Nurse examination accurately. We have found points where official publications differ or are silent. Could you please confirm each question below in writing, with the official document name, version and effective date, or confirm that the information is not published?

1. Which RGN duration controls: 180 minutes on the live regulator/provider tables, 210 in the linked blueprint/2020 circular, or 150 in FAQ 46? Please identify the superseding document and effective date.
2. Please distinguish timed answering time from check-in/tutorial time, breaks, total appointment time and any blocks.
3. Please confirm total/scored/pilot items and explain whether the 50% standard is raw, equated or scaled, how reports are issued and how §7.6 rounding operates.
4. Are RGN domain counts 30/61/44/15 current? Please distinguish RGN from Registered Midwife and Nurse Technician.
5. Which examination languages, centres and remote-delivery routes are expressly authorized for RGN? Please provide RN-specific authorization rather than another profession's arrangements.
6. Please date and confirm version 04 of the Qualifying Examination Policy, including five attempts, justified additional attempts, waiting/reset rules. Does the backup's three-attempt physician-section wording apply to RGN at all?
7. Please confirm three-year pass-result validity and the policy's evaluation/restoration/renewal exceptions, separately from licence/evaluation validity.
8. Which RGN preparation references and released sample items may be used? Is any past-question bank authorized, and what rules cover recalled live content?
