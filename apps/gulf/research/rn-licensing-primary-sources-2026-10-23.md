Read date: **23 October 2026 (Asia/Riyadh)**. Research branch: `codex/gulf-rn-regulatory-research-2026-10-23`.

Scope: DHA Dubai **Registered Nurse**; DOH Abu Dhabi **Registered Nurse**; Qatar DHP **Registered General Nurse**. SNLE is limited to reconfirming the currently linked applicant/content guide and identifying an inconsistency on its official linking page. This document changes no application code or SNLE files.

Only regulator publications and the named testing providers support findings. Search-engine publication estimates, URL folder dates, copyright footers, website interface languages and third-party summaries are not treated as examination policy dates or facts. **NOT FOUND OFFICIALLY** means not established in the official material accessible in this review; it does not prove that an internal or subsequently published document does not exist. A missing pilot-item statement does not mean there are no pilot items. Source IDs below resolve to the exact URL, title, document date, read date and a short verbatim quotation in the source list.
