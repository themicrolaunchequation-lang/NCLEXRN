# Reviewer brief — Gulf nursing editions (draft, not for release)

Who: a registered nurse educator familiar with the target regulator (SCFHS, DHA, DOH or DHP).
Nothing here is approved. No question may reach learners until it is signed off for that regulator.

## Files (apps/gulf/reports/generated/)
- `question-crosswalk-review-queue.csv`: all 1,350 questions with `review_priority`.
- `core-spot-check-sample.csv`: 57 items from the "clean Core" pool, to test whether the screen can be trusted.
- `saudi-specific-wording.csv`: 6 items to rewrite or drop.

## Order of work
1. Priority 1 (6 items): rewrite or exclude for your regulator.
2. Priority 2 (95 items): jurisdiction signals (units, drug names, vaccination, escalation, cultural care). Decide keep / adapt / drop.
3. Core spot-check (57 items): mark each correct, needs change, or unsafe. If more than 5% need change, the whole Core pool (949) is re-reviewed.
4. Priority 3 (300 items): policy/scope wording. Keep only if the answer holds under your regulator.

## For each item record
verdict (APPROVE / ADAPT / DROP), reasons, your name, date, and the official reference URL that supports the answer.
Do not copy text from real exam papers or exam-prep banks.
