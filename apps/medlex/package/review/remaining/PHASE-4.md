# Phase 4 — Screens

Rebuilt Today around one daily-session action, compact module/draft continuation,
a weekly rhythm and a concise word of the day. Learn has status filters and
path/grid/system views. Modules now have Overview, Words, Practice, Activities
and Resources, with all original outcomes/tasks/references retained.

Library uses 100-row paging, A–Z/filter/sort/list-grid controls, desktop preview,
and full-page word profiles with previous/next and related roots. Practice has
seven consistent mode cards and three applied reflection areas. Progress separates
objective skill evidence, self-rated familiarity and portfolio work. Teach retains
assignments, packs, curriculum mapping, editorial overlays and sourced research.

Primary implementation files: `src/features/modern/views.js`, `src/features/library/index.js`, `src/features/worksheet/index.js`.

Development checkpoint: `phase-checkpoints/Phase-4.html` in the review ZIP.
Final integrated QA and size measurements are in `validation.json`; the final
release supersedes these intermediate checkpoints.
