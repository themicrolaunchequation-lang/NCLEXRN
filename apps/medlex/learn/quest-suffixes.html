What is a suffix?

A suffix is a word part placed at the end of a word. It may describe a condition, symptom, procedure, specialist or a quality such as “pertaining to”.

Check the ending. Ask: what does it tell me?

✦

Why learn suffixes?

Similar-looking words can describe very different things. Recognising an ending helps you distinguish what is happening from what is being examined or treated.

A helpful clue—not a substitute for the complete meaning.

What is a medical suffix?

A suffix is the ending of a medical word — a useful clue to its meaning. The root usually names a body part; the suffix tells you what is happening to it and what kind of word you are reading: a condition? an operation? a test? a person?
