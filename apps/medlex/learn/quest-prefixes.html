What is a prefix?

A prefix is a word part placed before a root or base. It can add information about position, time, number, degree or absence. Not every medical term has one.

Look at the beginning. Ask: what detail does it add?

✦

Why learn prefixes?

You begin to recognise relationships instead of memorising each word alone. Spot hyper- and hypo-, for example, and you already have a clue about high and low.

A helpful clue—not a substitute for the complete meaning.
