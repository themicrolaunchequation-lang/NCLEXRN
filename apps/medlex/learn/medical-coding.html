04 / From care to useful data

What medical coding does

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

Represents documented care

Transforms recorded diagnoses, procedures and other reportable information into standardised codes.

Supports reporting

Makes patterns of illness and healthcare activity easier to aggregate and compare.

Supports operations

Contributes to service planning, audit, quality measurement and reimbursement.

Preserves traceability

Each assigned code should be explainable from the record and the applicable rules.

A code is a structured representation of information. It does not replace the full clinical record or establish a diagnosis by itself.

05 / Related roles, different tasks

Documentation, coding & billing

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

Clinical documentation

Clinicians record the assessment, findings, decisions and care provided.

Medical coding

Coding professionals abstract reportable information and apply classification rules.

Billing & claims

Teams submit the required financial and administrative data and manage payer responses.

Clinical documentation integrity (CDI) helps improve record clarity and reliability. Accurate coding contributes to billing, but a valid code does not guarantee coverage or payment.

07 / A useful conceptual map

Classification & terminology differ

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

Clinical terminology

SNOMED CT represents clinical concepts for recording and exchanging health information.

Classification

ICD groups conditions into categories for reporting, analysis and other specified uses.

A terminology concept and a classification code are not automatically interchangeable. A mapping can be incomplete, context-dependent or one-to-many.

12 / US ICD-10-CM comparison

Uncertainty: the setting matters

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

Outpatient

Do not report an uncertain diagnosis as established. Report the highest degree of certainty supported for that visit.

Eligible hospital inpatient discharge

Certain uncertain diagnoses documented at discharge may be coded as established under the inpatient guideline.

These rules are setting-specific. Read Sections II.H and IV.H, including exceptions. Observation care remains outpatient unless the person is admitted as an inpatient.

13 / Which code comes first?

Sequencing is part of the meaning

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

Principal diagnosis

In US hospital inpatient coding: the condition established after study as chiefly responsible for admission.

First-listed condition

In US outpatient coding: the condition or reason chiefly responsible for the services, subject to the guidelines.

Sequence according to the applicable rules. The most severe condition or highest-paying code is not automatically first.

14 / US systems in context

Procedure & service coding

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

ICD-10-PCS

Used for reporting inpatient hospital procedures in the US.

CPT® / HCPCS Level I

Describes professional services and procedures, including many outpatient services.

HCPCS Level II

Identifies additional items and services, including supplies, equipment and certain drugs.

The diagnosis code explains the reportable condition or reason. A procedure or service code describes what was done. Select the code set for the specific reporting context.

17 / Professional judgment

Accuracy, ethics & technology

Explore the detail panels. Retrieve the meaning. Explain the distinction.

Stay evidence-based

Represent supported care. Do not increase or suppress coding to distort the record or payment.

Query responsibly

Use relevant clinical indicators and neutral wording when clarification is needed.

Review automated suggestions

AI and encoders assist the workflow. Their suggestions still need documentation and rule checks.

Protect confidentiality

Use approved systems and handle records according to applicable privacy requirements.
