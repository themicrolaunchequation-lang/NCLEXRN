YouTube description:
Your process is stable, so why are results still outside specification? Cp and Cpk answer it.
In this video: the car-in-the-garage analogy, the Cp and Cpk formulas, a 3D view of a centred process (LSL 0, USL 12, mean 6, σ 1: Cp 2.00, Cpk 2.00) and what happens when the mean moves to 9 (Cp 2.00, Cpk 1.00), the "move it, tighten it, or change the limits?" decision, a live capability histogram (Cp 1.54, Cpk 1.49), the common 1.33 minimum, and where Cp/Cpk came from (Juran, Kane 1986).
