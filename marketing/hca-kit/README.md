# Healthcare Crown Academy: marketing kit

- `video-pipeline/`: the system that makes the promo videos. `out/` holds all finished videos. `kit/AGENT-PROMPT.md` explains how to re-make or edit them. The voice model files are not stored here (they are too big); download `kokoro-v1.0.onnx` and `voices-v1.0.bin` from the kokoro-onnx releases page.
- `publishing-pack/`: copy for YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn; the guide PDF; LinkedIn thumbnails (`thumbs2/`, zipped per app in `tz2/`); and the scheduling CSVs (`csv/`, including the Buffer ones).
- The other account's videos are in `marketing/videos/`.
