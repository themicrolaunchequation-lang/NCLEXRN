What the data says

  • Exact niche terms are tiny on YouTube: “SNLE exam”, “DHA exam for nurses”, “SBAR nursing”, “medical terminology” and “nursing awards” each show under 750 searches a month. That is good news, because the competition is also low (12–34), so a well-titled video can rank near the top. Always pair the niche term with one broad, high-volume term in the title or first line.
  • Best opportunities (high volume, low competition): “lean six sigma healthcare” (5.3k, competition 30), “operational excellence” (8.7k, 27), “nurse burnout” (6.6k, 30), “burnout recovery” (64k, 39), “how to lock in studying” (21.7k, 30), “green belt” (7.2k, rising +80%), “six sigma” (20k, rising +83%), “medical terms” (8.1k, rising +148%), “nclex review” (9.9k, rising +84%).
  • Gulf: most searchers come from the source countries (India, the Philippines, Egypt and others), so English titles that name the exam (SNLE, DHA, DOH, DHP, Prometric) reach them. Arabic titles and hashtags reach candidates already in the Gulf.
