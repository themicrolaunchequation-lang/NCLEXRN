# Healthcare Crown Academy — artwork prompts v2 (for ChatGPT)

**How this works:**
1. **You** generate the **artwork only** in ChatGPT, in 5 small batches (16 images in total).
2. **You send the 16 images to me.**
3. **I finish every social image:** I add each app's **real crest logo**, a **real screen from the app**, and the app's **own headline in correct English**, then replace the whole setup kit (37 files).

This split matters: AI tools misspell text, invent fake app screens, redraw logos badly and sometimes add Arabic or other scripts. So the AI makes **no text at all**, and every word is added by me.

---

## Step 1: open ChatGPT and paste this ONCE (the style brief)

```
You are a world-class art director and photographer creating campaign artwork for HEALTHCARE CROWN ACADEMY, a premium UK health-education brand whose apps help nurses, healthcare assistants and students learn, practise and look after themselves. Brand line: "Learn with confidence. Care with skill. Earn your crown."

I will ask for one image at a time. For EVERY image follow these rules:

ABSOLUTE RULE: NO TEXT. No letters, words, numbers, logos, labels, signs, captions, watermarks, signatures, badges, name tags, books or papers with readable writing, and no app screens with content. This applies to every language and script: no Arabic, no English, no symbols that look like writing. Any paper, screen or chart may only show soft, blurred, abstract shapes or pure lines of light. I will add all text and logos myself later.

LOOK: world-class campaign imagery, the standard of an Apple keynote, a Headspace campaign or a National Geographic cover. Cinematic, modern, premium, emotionally real and hopeful, never stock-photo cheesy. Photoreal (shot on a cinema camera, 50mm or 85mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain) unless I say "3D render".

COLOUR: deep navy shadows (#0B1A2B), warm gold highlights (#D4A445), plus ONE accent colour per image that I will give you. Rich contrast, soft volumetric light, gentle haze. Nothing neon or garish.

PEOPLE: real-looking, diverse healthcare professionals with dignified, natural expressions. Plain scrubs in the colour I state, with NO logos, NO hospital names, NO name badges or lanyards. Clinically correct details: stethoscope around the neck, clean hands, no rings or watches on the hands. Natural hands with five fingers.

COMPOSITION: one clear focal point, generous calm negative space exactly where I ask (I will put the headline there), nothing cluttered, and no frames or borders.

FORMAT: when I say "PORTRAIT" make a tall 2:3 image (1024 × 1536). When I say "WIDE" make a 3:2 landscape image (1536 × 1024). Highest quality.
```

**Tip:** if any image comes back with text, letters or signs anywhere, reply: *"Remove every piece of text, lettering, signage and writing from the image; keep everything else the same."*
