# HCA Content Studio: make your own content, at low cost

## Your toolkit (all free to start)

| Job | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Write posts, emails, scripts and replies | **Claude** (your Pro plan) with a Project set up from these files | Already paid |
| Schedule posts (LinkedIn, Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) | **Buffer** free plan: 3 channels, 10 posts each, with its built-in AI assistant for quick rewrites | £0 |
| Images and simple videos | **Canva** free: unlimited templates, limited AI credits | £0 |
| Short-video editing and auto-captions | **CapCut** free: auto-captions and templates | £0 |
| Personalised outreach emails | **Gmail + YAMM** free: about 20–50 a day from a Google Sheet | £0 |

## Set up Claude once (5 minutes)

1. Go to claude.ai → **Projects** → **Create project**. Name it "HCA Content Studio".
2. Open **Instructions** and paste in everything from `1-PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
3. Open **Project knowledge** and upload `2-KNOWLEDGE-PACK.md`.
4. Keep `3-PROMPT-LIBRARY.md` open, and copy a prompt into a new chat in that project.
5. When a price or feature changes, edit the knowledge pack and upload it again.

## A typical week (about 2 hours in total)

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Run the "Weekly batch" prompt. Paste the posts into Buffer and the emails into your YAMM sheet. Make 3 images in Canva. | 60 min |
| Tuesday to Friday | Hand-post in Facebook exam groups and Reddit. Send the day's emails in YAMM. Reply to comments using the "Replies" prompt. | 15 min a day |
| Friday | Check which ?ref= links brought visitors, and do more of what works. | 5 min |
