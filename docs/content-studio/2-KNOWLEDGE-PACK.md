## The apps

### MedLex: understand medical words
- **Link:** https://medlex.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk
- **Who it's for:** nursing and allied health students, healthcare assistants, clinical coders, and international nurses learning medical English.
- **What it does:**
  - Decode medical terms from their parts (prefix + root + suffix), for example tachy + card + ia.
  - 26 modules, 1,480 terms and 56 illustrated guides, with spaced practice.
  - Word-part studios for prefixes, roots and suffixes, plus a pronunciation practice tool.
  - Works offline once opened.
- **Free guide:** https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/medical-word-parts

### Lumen: study your own notes
- **Link:** https://lumen.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk
- **Who it's for:** students with lecture slides, PDFs and photos of notes.
- **What it does:**
  - Read and highlight your own PDFs and photos, and turn what matters into recall cards with spaced practice.
  - A focus timer, planner, board and lab values reference.
  - The library stays on your device.
  - AI help only if you add your own key.
- **Free guides:**
  - https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/study/active-recall-for-nursing-students
  - https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/study/spaced-repetition-study-plan
  - https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/study/turn-lecture-slides-into-flashcards
  - https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/study/exam-week-study-plan

### Carecraft: everyday care skills
- **Link:** https://carecraft.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk
- **Who it's for:** nurses, healthcare assistants, care-home staff, preceptors and practice educators.
- **What it does:**
  - A 60-second scenario a day, 30 short lessons, 32 practice activities and 18 toolkit job aids (for example an SBAR handover builder).
  - Topics include communication, safety, teamwork, escalation and handover.
  - Scenarios are fictional, and it is not CPD.
- **Free guides:**
  - https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/carecraft
  - …/guides/carecraft/sbar
  - …/guides/carecraft/communication
  - …/guides/carecraft/safety
  - …/guides/carecraft/empathy
  - …/guides/carecraft/infection
  - …/guides/carecraft/reasoning

### Serein: a little room to breathe (free for everyone)
- **Link:** https://serein.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk
- **Who it's for:** nurses, carers and students on hard days.
- **What it does:**
  - 15 short, private practices for before, during and after a shift: breathing, grounding, movement and reflection.
  - Nothing is monitored.
  - "Get help" lists UK, Saudi, UAE and Qatar crisis lines.
  - It is self-care, not treatment.
- **Free guide:** https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/serein

### Gulf Region Nursing Licensure Studio: prepare for your licence
- **Link:** https://snle.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk
- **Who it's for:** nurses sitting the Saudi SNLE, Qatar DHP, Dubai DHA or Abu Dhabi DOH exam. Many are from the Philippines, India, Egypt, Jordan, Pakistan and Nigeria.
- **What it does:**
  - 1,297 clinically reviewed practice questions, 67 clinical reasoning tools, 35 specialty dossiers and a bilingual glossary.
  - Mock exams with tutor and exam modes, and a review of misses.
  - Each exam keeps its own progress.
- **Must say:** original practice material, not official examination questions, and not endorsed by SCFHS, DHP, DHA or DOH.
- **Free guides:**
  - https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/gulf/compare-gulf-nursing-exams
  - …/guides/gulf/saudi-snle-nursing-exam
  - …/guides/gulf/qatar-dhp-nursing-exam
  - …/guides/gulf/dubai-dha-nursing-exam
  - …/guides/gulf/abu-dhabi-doh-nursing-exam

### Evinca: your best work, recognised on evidence
- **Link:** https://evinca.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/en
- **Who it's for:** nurses and healthcare staff, and the patients and families who want to thank them.
- **What it does:**

- **Must say:** not a licence to practise, a regulatory registration or an academic qualification.
- **Free guide:** https://healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk/guides/how-to-thank-a-nurse

## Channel rules

| Channel | Rules |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Founder voice. Polls and short stories do best. 1 link, in the post or the first comment. Up to 3 hashtags. |
| **Facebook exam groups** | Ask the admin first. Lead with a free "question of the day" with its reasoning. Never post the same text in many groups on the same day. Say you're the founder. |
| **Reddit** | Answer the question fully first, then add the link only if allowed. Read each subreddit's rules, and use promo threads where they exist. No hashtags. Never ask for upvotes. |
| **Email** (outreach to organisations) | Personal and short (under 120 words). One ask. Say who you are and why them. Include "Reply 'no thanks' and I won't email again." Only to work addresses; sole traders need consent. |
| **TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels** | A hook in the first 2 seconds, 15–45 seconds long, on-screen captions, and one idea per video. |
| **YouTube (long)** | One question explained in 3–6 minutes. The title includes the exam name. Link the matching guide in the description. |
