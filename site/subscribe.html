Questions, answered

Which one is for me?

Studying or working in healthcare anywhere: the Crown Pass. Sitting the Saudi, Qatar, Dubai or Abu Dhabi nursing exam: the Gulf Pass, which already includes the Crown Pass. Leading quality improvement: the Green Belt.

Can I buy just one app?

No, and you do not need to. The Crown Pass gives you every app for one small price, and each app has a free version you can keep using without paying.

I am taking more than one Gulf exam. Do I pay twice?

No. One Gulf Pass covers all four exams: Saudi SNLE, Qatar DHP, Dubai DHA and Abu Dhabi DOH. Each exam keeps its own progress.

How does the free trial work?

The Crown Pass starts with 7 days free. You add a card and pay £0 today. Your first payment is taken on day 8 and the plan then renews automatically. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. Your 14-day cancellation period starts on the day you start the trial, and you are never charged for trial days.

What is the founding price?

Join the yearly Crown Pass before 31 January 2027 and your first year is £29.99. After that it renews at £39.99 a year, and we will remind you before it does. From 1 February 2027 the yearly Crown Pass is £49.99 for new members.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, in two ways. Stop renewal at any time: your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, with no refund. Or, within 14 days of buying or starting your trial, cancel and get a refund: your access ends now and you get a full refund. The Gulf Pass and the Green Belt are paid once and never renew. Your progress is never deleted.

Why is the Crown Pass cheaper in my country?

Crown Pass prices follow World Bank income groups so the Academy is fair everywhere. Upper-middle-income countries pay less, and lower-income countries pay least, in US dollars. The Gulf Pass and the Green Belt cost the same everywhere. Stripe may offer your local currency at checkout.
