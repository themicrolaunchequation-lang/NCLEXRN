Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

The Crown Pass starts with 7 days free. You add a card, pay £0 today, and can cancel before the trial ends without being charged.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Use “Manage or cancel my plan” below. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and your progress is never deleted.

Why is the price different in my country?

Prices follow World Bank income groups so the Academy is fair everywhere. Upper-middle-income countries pay less, and lower-income countries pay least, in US dollars. Gulf Region exam passes cost the same everywhere.

Can I pay in my own currency?

Stripe may offer your local currency at checkout. You can always choose to pay in the plan’s own currency instead.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, and you can do it yourself with “Request a refund” below. You get a full refund within 3 days of paying. Within 14 days you get back the unused part. Full details are on our subscriptions and refunds page.
