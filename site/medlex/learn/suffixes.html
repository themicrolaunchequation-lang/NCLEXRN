What is a Medical Suffix?

Explore the detail panels, retrieve the meaning, then explain the distinction.

A suffix is the ending of a medical word that classifies it and usually indicates:

  • Condition or Disease (-itis, -osis, -pathy)
  • Procedure or Surgery (-ectomy, -otomy, -plasty)
  • Diagnostic Test (-scopy, -graphy, -gram)
  • Specialist or Field (-logist, -ology, -ian)
  • Formation or Growth (-poiesis, -plasia, -blast)

Practical Application

Explore the detail panels, retrieve the meaning, then explain the distinction.

Patient Chart

Diagnosis: The patient has pneumonia with hypoxemia and requires thoracentesis .

Decoding:

  • Pneumonia: pneumon (lung) + -ia (condition) = lung condition/inflammation
  • Hypoxemia: hypo- (low) + ox (oxygen) + -emia (blood condition) = low oxygen in blood
  • Thoracentesis: thorac (chest) + -centesis (puncture to withdraw) = chest puncture to withdraw fluid

Surgical Report

Procedure: The patient underwent cholecystectomy with laparoscopy .

Decoding:

  • Cholecystectomy: cholecyst (gallbladder) + -ectomy (removal) = gallbladder removal
  • Laparoscopy: laparo (abdomen) + -scopy (visual examination) = abdominal visualization

Lab Results

Findings: Leukocytosis with thrombocytopenia and hyperglycemia .

Decoding:

  • Leukocytosis: leuk/o (white) + cyt (cell) + -osis (abnormal condition) = high white blood cell count
  • Thrombocytopenia: thromb/o (clot) + cyt (cell) + -penia (deficiency) = low platelet count
  • Hyperglycemia: hyper- (high) + glyc (sugar) + -emia (blood condition) = high blood sugar
