Free trial

The Crown Pass starts with a 7-day free trial. You are not charged if you cancel before the trial ends. If you cancel during the trial with “Cancel and get a refund”, the plan ends at once and nothing is taken. Your contract starts on the day you start the trial, so your 14-day cancellation period also starts then. A trial turning into a paid plan does not start a new 14-day period. If you then cancel within the 14 days, you are never charged for the free trial days; only paid days count.

Your 14-day right to cancel

If you are a consumer, you can cancel a new plan or exam pass without giving a reason. Normally, your cancellation period ends at the end of the 14th day after the day your contract starts (the day you buy, or the day you start a free trial). For example, if you buy on 1 March, you can cancel up to the end of 15 March. It can last longer if the law requires, including if we have not given you the required cancellation information. Your confirmation email shows the normal deadline. You can cancel by any clear message sent before the deadline.

At checkout we ask whether you want your access to start straight away, during the cancellation period. You tick a box to say so; it is separate from any marketing choice, and we record the wording and the time you agreed.

What you get back

  • Within 3 days (72 hours) of your payment: if you cancel in that time, we refund that payment in full under our own three-day guarantee. It does not shorten your cancellation period or reduce any legal remedy. We send the refund without undue delay and within the legal deadline; your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • After that, within your 14-day period: we deduct only a fair proportion for the paid access actually supplied before you cancelled, and only because you asked us to start straight away and we gave you the required information (otherwise you get a full refund). The deduction is the price you actually paid × the paid time supplied ÷ the full paid period, using that period's real start and end dates; we round only the final amount, add no fees, and show you the amount before you confirm. Free trial days are never charged. Example: a £39.99 yearly plan (365 days) cancelled 6 days after payment: £39.99 × 6/365 = £0.66 kept, £39.33 back. For a 3-month exam pass, the calculation uses the pass length. If the law gives you more, we will pay the higher amount.
  • If you did not ask for access to start during the cancellation period, you get a full refund.
  • We never charge administration fees for cancelling.

How to cancel

  • Fastest: while signed in, use “Cancel and get a refund” on the plans page or your account page. It shows the amount first; when you confirm, it sends the refund and ends your paid access. We then email you an acknowledgement with the date, when your access ended and how the refund was worked out. If the payment system fails, your cancellation still counts from when you submitted it, and we sort out the refund separately.
  • Or tell us another clear way, for example by emailing themicrolaunchequation@gmail.com with “I cancel my Healthcare Crown Academy plan” and the email address on your account. You may use the model form below, but you do not have to.

We refund to the card you paid with, without undue delay and at the latest within 14 days of the day you tell us. Card refunds usually reach your account within 5 to 10 working days of being sent.

The instant “Cancel and get a refund” button covers one refund per person for each product. If you have used it before, or it cannot help, email us before the deadline: we will apply the same legal cancellation rules and refund deadline, and treat the day you sent your email as your cancellation date. The button limit never limits your legal rights.

After the 14 days

Once your cancellation period has ended, you can still stop renewal at any time, but we do not refund part-used periods simply because you change your mind. Under the rules that apply today, an ordinary automatic renewal of the same contract does not usually start a new 14-day cancellation period. Different rights can apply if a new contract is made, or when the new UK subscription rules start; we will apply any rights that cover your contract and update this page before then.

Everything we supply must be as described, of satisfactory quality and fit for purpose, and our services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill. If a service is not, you can normally ask us to perform it again at our cost; if that is impossible, or is not done within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience, a price reduction may be due, up to a full refund. Faulty digital content has separate repair, replacement and price-reduction rights. Other legal remedies, including compensation where available, are not excluded. If we charge you twice, charge you after you cancelled, or charge you a price you did not agree, we will refund it. Tell us at themicrolaunchequation@gmail.com.

Renewals and reminders

  • Monthly plans renew each month and yearly plans each year until you stop renewal.
  • Before a yearly plan renews, and before a free trial turns into a paid plan, we send you a reminder with the date, the amount and how to stop it.
  • New UK rules for subscriptions are expected to start on 1 January 2027, subject to Parliament’s approval. They will add more reminders and, for some renewals, a further cancellation period. We will update this page and our emails before they apply.

Price changes

The price you agreed is fixed for the period you have paid for. If we change a plan’s price, the new price applies only from your next renewal, and we will email you at least 30 days before that renewal so you can stop renewal first at no cost. The founding price (£29.99) is for your first year only. Your plan then renews at £39.99 a year unless you stop it, and we will remind you before it renews.

Changes to what a plan includes

We improve the apps often. We will not take away a main feature of a paid plan during a period you have paid for, unless we have to for a legal, security or supplier reason outside our control. If that happens and the change is to your disadvantage, we will tell you, and you may cancel and receive a refund of the unused part of your payment.

If we suspend or end your plan

We may suspend or end a plan only for the reasons in our terms. If we end it without it being your fault, we refund the unused part. If we suspend paid access while we review a concern, we will not charge you for access you cannot use; if a renewal is taken in error, we will refund it.
