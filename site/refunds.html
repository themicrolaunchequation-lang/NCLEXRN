Subscriptions & refunds

Last updated: 26 October 2026

How billing works

  • You can pay monthly or yearly. Before you pay we show you the price, what it covers, the billing period, the date of the first renewal and how to cancel.
  • Prices are in pounds sterling. Where VAT applies, it is included in the price shown.
  • Plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel. For yearly plans we will email you a reminder before the renewal date, with the price and a link to cancel.
  • If a plan includes a free trial, you will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. We will tell you the end date and the price that follows.

Cancel any time

You can cancel at any time from your account, or by emailing us. Cancelling is as easy as signing up. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for and then it will not renew. Your free features, and everything you have written or saved so far, stay yours.

Your 14-day cancellation right

If you are a consumer in the UK, you can cancel a new subscription within 14 days of buying it, without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. The 14 days start on the day you buy.

Our apps are online services you can use straight away. At checkout we will ask you to confirm that you want access to start immediately and that you understand how this affects your cancellation right. If you ask for access to start and then cancel within the 14 days, you will pay a proportionate amount for the time you have already had, and we will refund the rest. If you did not ask for access to start, you will get a full refund.

To cancel within the 14 days, use the cancel option in your account or email hello@healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk with a clear statement that you want to cancel, such as: “I cancel my Healthcare Crown Academy subscription”, together with the email address on your account. We will refund what you are owed without undue delay and in any case within 14 days of the day you tell us, using the payment method you paid with.

After 14 days

Outside the 14 days we do not normally refund part-used periods, and your plan simply will not renew. If something has gone wrong, for example you were charged twice, you were charged after you cancelled, or a paid feature does not work as described, please tell us and we will put it right, including a refund where that is fair. Your statutory rights, such as the right to a repair, replacement or refund for digital content that is faulty or not as described, are not affected.

Evinca recognition assessment fees are covered by the terms shown when you apply.

Price changes

If we change the price of a plan, we will tell you at least 30 days before it applies to your next renewal, so that you can cancel first.

Questions and complaints

Email hello@healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk with the email address on your account and, if you have it, your receipt number. We aim to reply within 5 working days. If we cannot settle a complaint, we will tell you, and you may also use an alternative dispute resolution provider.
