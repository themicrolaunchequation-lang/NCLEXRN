    FAQ

    Billing and the basics.

    Anything else? Email hello@healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk.

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes. Cancel from your account or by emailing us. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, then it does not renew. Your free features and your progress stay yours.

    What is the 14-day cancellation right?

    If you are a consumer in the UK, you can cancel a new subscription within 14 days of buying it for a full refund, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. Because the apps are digital content you can use straight away, we ask at checkout whether you want access to start immediately; if you agree and then cancel within the 14 days, we refund the unused part and keep only a proportionate amount for the time you have already had. Read subscriptions & refunds.

    What is free?

    Without paying you get MedLex modules 1 to 3, the Carecraft daily scenario and 6 lessons, Serein starter practices and Get help, Lumen study tools with a daily AI allowance, and a free Evinca story. No card is needed.

    Is there student or team pricing?

    Email hello@healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk with your course or the size of your team and we will tell you what we can offer. Discount codes can be entered at checkout when plans open.

    What happens to my progress and my privacy?

    MedLex, Carecraft and Serein keep your progress, notes and settings in your browser, on your device. If you sign in, Gulf Nursing and Evinca save to your account, and Lumen's Academy AI sends only the text you choose to our AI service. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies and there are no ads. If you sign in, one strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in across the apps. Read the privacy notice.

    Which devices does it work on?

    Any up-to-date browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can also add each app to your home screen: on iPhone or iPad tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android and desktop Chrome or Edge use Install.

    Does it work offline?

    MedLex keeps working offline once you have opened it, Carecraft has a downloadable offline edition and Serein can be saved for offline use from its settings. Lumen keeps your library on your device; AI help needs a connection.

    What is Evinca?

    Evinca is our recognition platform. You can share the story of your work for free and, when you are ready, apply for a paid evidence-based review. Evinca recognition is not a licence to practise, a regulatory registration or an academic qualification.
