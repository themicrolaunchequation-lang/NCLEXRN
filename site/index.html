FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Email themicrolaunchequation@gmail.com.

What is Healthcare Crown Academy?

HEALTHCARE CROWN ACADEMY LTD is a UK-registered company (No. 17501904) dedicated to healthcare education, professional development, innovation and recognition. We build digital learning, self-paced courses and interactive tools for healthcare professionals worldwide.

What is Evinca?

Evinca is our international digital platform for celebrating and recognising excellence in healthcare. Professionals, teams and institutions can share their achievements and apply for evidence-based recognition through a structured review.

Is sharing my story really free?

Yes. Professional storytelling on Evinca is free. Recognition assessments are optional and paid. You'll always see the fee before you apply.

How does the recognition review work?

You submit an application with supporting evidence. It's reviewed through a structured process against published criteria covering evidence, impact and professional values. Approved applicants receive a digital recognition record and certificate.

Who can take part?

Healthcare professionals, teams and institutions in any country, including nurses, midwives, physicians, pharmacists, allied health professionals, paramedics, educators, researchers and healthcare leaders.

Is Evinca recognition a licence or a qualification?

No. Evinca recognition is an independent professional recognition. It is not a licence to practise, a regulatory registration or an academic qualification, and it does not replace the requirements of your professional regulator.

Can I install this as an app?

Yes. On Android and desktop Chrome or Edge, use the Install app button. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
