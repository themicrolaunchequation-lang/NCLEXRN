Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by HEALTHCARE CROWN ACADEMY LTD, a private company limited by shares, registered in England and Wales with company number 17501904 (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this notice.

Contact: hello@healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk

What we collect

  • When you join Evinca or contact us: your name, email address and, if you choose to give them, your role, organisation, interests and message.
  • When you subscribe to updates: your email address and where on the site you subscribed.
  • When you browse: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

The “recognition record” preview and the readiness check run entirely in your browser. What you type there is not sent to us.

How and why we use it

  • To reply to you and set up your interest in Evinca, our courses or a partnership. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To send you updates about Evinca and new courses, only if you tick the box or subscribe. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing us.
  • To keep the website secure and running. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests.

We never sell your personal data.

Your account and plans

If you create an account we hold your email address, your name and the details you choose to add (such as profession or country), your sign-in session, and which plan or access you have. We use this to run your account across the website and apps. Our lawful basis is performing our contract with you. If you reserve a plan, we keep your email and the plan you chose so we can tell you when it opens.

When paid plans open, payments will be handled by Stripe, our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card details. We keep a record of your subscription and receipts for as long as the law requires for accounting, normally six years.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf under contract:

  • Supabase, which stores form submissions in its London (United Kingdom) data centre region.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and serves it through a global network, and Cloudflare, which manages our domain name settings.

Where a provider processes data outside the UK, we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Nothing we store is used to track you.

  • Account cookie (hca-auth): if you sign in, we set a strictly necessary cookie named hca-auth (split into parts such as hca-auth.0) on .healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk. It holds your sign-in session so one account works across this site and every app subdomain. It lasts up to a year, or until you sign out.
  • App progress: MedLex, Carecraft, Serein and Lumen each keep your progress, notes and settings in your browser's local storage on their own subdomain (for example medlex.healthcarecrownacademy.co.uk). This stays on your device; clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Preferences and offline use: your light or dark theme choice and, if you install an app, a copy of it for offline use.

Lumen and AI providers

Lumen's optional AI features can use an AI provider key that you add yourself (for example from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google). Your key is stored in your browser, and your questions and the source text you choose are sent directly from your browser to that provider. They do not pass through our servers, and the provider's own privacy terms apply to that processing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you. We keep subscriber emails until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email us. We will reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

We'll update this page if how we use personal data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
