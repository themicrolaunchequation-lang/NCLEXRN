Medical prefixes, suffixes and roots

Most medical words are built from a few small parts. Learn the parts once and you can read words you have never seen before.

tachy fast+card heart+ia condition=tachycardia: a fast heart rate

Quick check: 8 common parts

Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does cardi/o mean?

cardi/o means heart, as in cardiology.

What does -itis mean?

-itis means inflammation, as in dermatitis.

What does tachy- mean?

tachy- means fast, as in tachycardia.

What does brady- mean?

brady- means slow, as in bradycardia.

What does -ectomy mean?

-ectomy means surgical removal, as in appendectomy.

What does hepat/o mean?

hepat/o means liver, as in hepatomegaly.

What does hyper- mean?

hyper- means above normal or excessive, as in hyperglycaemia.

What does -algia mean?

-algia means pain, as in neuralgia.

Find a word part

UK and US English spell some parts differently, for example -aemia and -emia, or haemat/o and hemat/o. Both mean the same.
