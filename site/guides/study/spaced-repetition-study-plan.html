Study skills

Spaced repetition: a simple study plan

Review just before you forget: how to space your revision so it lasts until the exam.

How to do it

  1. Learn a topic today and test yourself on it.
  2. Review it again after 1 day.
  3. Then after about 3 days, then a week, then a few weeks.
  4. If you get a card wrong, bring it back sooner.

Why it works

Spacing reviews out takes less total time than cramming and lasts longer. A card system can schedule this for you so you only see what is due.
