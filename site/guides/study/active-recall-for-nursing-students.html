Study skills

Active recall for nursing students

Test yourself instead of rereading: a simple method to remember lectures for longer.

How to do it

  1. Close your notes and write down everything you remember about one topic.
  2. Open your notes and mark what you missed or got wrong.
  3. Turn each gap into a short question you can answer in one line.
  4. Answer those questions again tomorrow, without looking first.

Why it works

Rereading feels productive because the page looks familiar. Trying to remember is harder, and that effort is what makes memory stick. Use your own lecture slides as the source, so what you practise matches what you are taught.
