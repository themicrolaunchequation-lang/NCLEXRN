Carecraft · Professional accountability

Own the next action

Make commitments visible, surface limits and follow through.

What you will practise

  • Be clear about responsibility
  • Make limits explicit
  • Close the commitment

Key point: Accountability includes saying when a task needs help.

Try a real situation

A senior colleague asks you, a support worker, to update a patient’s care plan after a risk assessment. Only a registered nurse can sign that off, and she has to rush to a call bell. What is the accountable response?

A. Do it carefully using your best judgement, as it needs doing and she has asked you.

B. Say you will have a look at it, then leave it unless she brings it up again later.

C. Say it is outside your role, ask who can do it, and make sure that person is told.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
