Carecraft · Debriefing

Turn an event into team learning

Compare expectations with events, identify conditions and close the learning loop.

What you will practise

  • Create a respectful purpose
  • Compare plan and reality
  • Choose a follow-through

Key point: A debrief needs both curiosity and follow-through.

Try a real situation

After a stressful resuscitation simulation, the team leader opens the debrief. The team is tired and a little tense, and two members are checking the time. Which question opens a useful debrief?

A. “What went wrong, and who needs to do something differently next time?”

B. “What did we expect to happen, and what actually happened as the scenario unfolded?”

C. “Did everyone feel it went well enough for us to move on to the next scenario?”

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
