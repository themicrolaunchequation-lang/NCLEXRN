Carecraft · Documentation

Write so the next person can act

Separate facts, decisions and responsibility in a concise learning note.

What you will practise

  • Record what was observed
  • Make the plan actionable
  • Check the receiving view

Key point: A record should make the next action easier to understand.

Try a real situation

You pick up a patient’s notes at the start of a late shift. The last entry, written at 14:20 by a colleague who has gone home, reads: “Team aware. Follow up later.” You are not sure what, if anything, you should do. What is missing from the entry?

A. A fuller account of what was discussed with the team, and who was present at the time.

B. A named person who will act on it and a time by which it should happen.

C. A list of the staff who were told, so the team can see exactly who is already aware of it.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.

Evidence and guidance

  • Starmer et al. · NEJM · 2014
