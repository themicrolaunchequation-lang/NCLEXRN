Carecraft · Coaching & learning

Feedback that leads somewhere

Describe behaviour, invite perspective and agree a small next experiment.

What you will practise

  • Choose a useful moment
  • Be specific and curious
  • Agree a next step

Key point: Useful feedback gives someone something specific they can try.

Try a real situation

You supervise a student nurse who forgot to name an owner for a task at handover. You have ten minutes at the end of the shift, and she is tired and a little anxious. Which feedback is most actionable?

A. “At handover, nobody was named for the review. Let’s practise saying who will do it.”

B. “You need to be more careful with handovers, because the detail really matters in this job.”

C. “That was a decent effort overall, and with a bit more practice I’m sure you will get there.”

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
