Carecraft · Digital stewardship

Protect information. Preserve trust.

Choose approved channels, minimise disclosure and check digital outputs.

What you will practise

  • Know the purpose and channel
  • Minimise what is shared
  • Verify and report

Key point: Useful information still needs an authorised purpose and destination.

Try a real situation

A student nurse posts a learning example in a study group chat. It has no name, but says “a 34-year-old with a rare genetic condition at the small unit on Skye, admitted last Tuesday”. A classmate says it is anonymous. Is it?

A. Yes, because the name has been left out and a person’s age alone does not identify them.

B. Not necessarily, because rare details, place and dates together may point to one person.

C. Yes, provided it is only shared once and stays within a closed group of fellow students.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
