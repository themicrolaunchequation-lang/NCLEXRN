Carecraft · Digital practice

AI literacy, clinical responsibility

Use emerging tools thoughtfully. Verify outputs. Protect trust and privacy.

What you will practise

  • Know the tool and its limits
  • Protect sensitive information
  • Verify before relying

Key point: A convincing output is not verified evidence. Accountability does not transfer to the tool.

Try a real situation

A colleague pastes an AI-generated summary into the team chat before your medicines safety meeting. It says a recent trial shows a benefit and cites “Hartley et al., 2023”, which nobody recognises. What is the strongest verification step?

A. Ask the tool for the full reference and the relevant quote, and accept it if it can supply both.

B. Find the original paper through a library or database and check it says what the summary claims.

C. Check that the journal looks credible and the year fits, then share it with a note that it needs review.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
