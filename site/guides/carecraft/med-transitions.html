Carecraft · Medication safety

Medication lists across transitions

Recognise discrepancies and clarify the intended plan with the responsible team.

What you will practise

  • Compare credible accounts
  • Clarify, do not prescribe by inference
  • Communicate the verified plan

Key point: A discrepancy is a question to resolve, not an instruction to guess.

Try a real situation

A resident comes back to the care home after a hospital stay. A medicine for his blood pressure is on the discharge list but is not on the care home’s own record, and the senior carer asks you what that means. What can you conclude?

A. The hospital probably stopped it, so the care home record is right and the discharge list can be set aside.

B. The care home missed it, so it should be added now, since the hospital list is the more recent document.

C. There is a discrepancy that needs verifying with the pharmacist or prescriber before anything changes.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
