Carecraft · Respect & choice

Protect dignity in small moments

Ask permission, speak directly and make participation accessible.

What you will practise

  • Address the person
  • Make choice and privacy visible
  • Adapt the interaction

Key point: Respect is expressed through permission, privacy and participation.

Try a real situation

Mrs Adeyemi, who has mild hearing loss, attends clinic with her daughter. The daughter answers every question you ask, and Mrs Adeyemi has stopped looking up. You have a full waiting room. What supports her dignity?

A. Let the daughter answer, as she is used to speaking for her mother and it will save time.

B. Ask the daughter to wait outside, so that the answers come from Mrs Adeyemi on her own.

C. Speak directly to Mrs Adeyemi, and ask her how she would like her daughter to be involved.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
