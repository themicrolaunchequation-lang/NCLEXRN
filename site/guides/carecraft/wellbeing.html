Carecraft · Sustainable practice

Care for the person providing care

Notice strain, protect recovery, and build a practical support plan.

What you will practise

  • Notice the load without judgment
  • Make one feasible adjustment
  • Use support early

Key point: Sustainable care needs both personal support and working conditions that make safe practice possible.

Try a real situation

Aisha, a healthcare assistant, tells you she has not slept properly for weeks and keeps losing track mid-task. The shift has started and the ward is short-staffed. What addresses both the immediate and the continuing concern?

A. Suggest a quiet break and say you will check in with how she feels at the end of the shift.

B. Share the trust wellbeing leaflet and ask her to pick one idea from it to try this week.

C. Offer help now, like a short break, and encourage her to raise the workload with her manager.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
