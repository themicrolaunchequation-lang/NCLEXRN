Carecraft · Human factors

Design around human limits

Recognise interruptions, memory load and fragile systems before blaming people.

What you will practise

  • Look at work as it happens
  • Build a reliable support
  • Test for new burden

Key point: A reliable process supports people when attention and memory are stretched.

Try a real situation

On a medical ward, the end-of-shift check that bed alarms are switched on keeps being missed. Three reminder emails and a team talk have not changed it, and the night shift is starting to feel blamed. What is a useful next step?

A. Send a firmer reminder and ask each nurse to confirm in writing that they have read it.

B. Watch the task as it happens, ask staff where it gets difficult, and test a cue at that step.

C. Look at who was on shift when it was missed and arrange extra training for those nurses.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
