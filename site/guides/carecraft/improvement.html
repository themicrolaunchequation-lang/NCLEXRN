Carecraft · Quality improvement

Turn reflection into better systems

Learn from near misses and test small, measurable changes.

What you will practise

  • Describe the process, not a person to blame
  • Choose a small, safe test
  • Review and adapt

Key point: A useful reflection ends with a small action, an owner, and a way to learn whether it helped.

Try a real situation

Task owners are often not named at handover on your ward. The manager suggests adding a mandatory “owner” column to the handover sheet across the whole hospital next month. Which next step supports learning from a small test?

A. Roll the new column out across the service and ask staff for feedback after a month.

B. Try a limited change on one shift for two weeks, with a measure and a check for added time.

C. Repeat the handover refresher session and count how many staff attend.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
