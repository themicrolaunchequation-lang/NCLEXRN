Try a real situation

At a team meeting about changing visiting hours, a healthcare assistant who usually has plenty to say stays silent throughout. The manager is keen to finish on time and records a consensus. What can you infer?

A. They agree with the plan, otherwise they would have said so, as they usually do.

B. Their view is unknown, so create another way for them to contribute.

C. They have no strong view on the matter, so the meeting can go ahead as planned.

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.
