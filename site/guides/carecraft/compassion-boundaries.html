Carecraft · Sustainable compassion

Care with compassion and boundaries

Acknowledge distress while offering realistic support within your role.

What you will practise

  • Notice and acknowledge
  • Offer a bounded response
  • Include the working conditions

Key point: Compassion can be warm, practical and honest about limits.

Try a real situation

A distressed relative stops you in the corridor and asks you, a healthcare assistant, to promise that her mother will not die this week. You have no information about the prognosis. Which response is compassionate and realistic?

A. “I can see how frightened you are. I can’t promise that, but I can ask the nurse to talk to you.”

B. “I’m sure she will be fine. She is in the best place and we are looking after her very well.”

C. “Lots of families feel like this at first. It really helps if you can try to stay strong for her.”

The answer, with why each option is right or wrong, is in Carecraft.

Evidence and guidance

  • Hodkinson et al. · BMJ · 2022
