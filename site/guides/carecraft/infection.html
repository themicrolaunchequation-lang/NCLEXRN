Carecraft · Infection prevention

Infection prevention, every time

Turn standard precautions into consistent, situation-aware habits.

What to do

  1. Apply standard precautions to everyone. Do not wait for an infection diagnosis. Match hand hygiene, PPE, injection safety, and equipment handling to the task and anticipated exposure.
  2. Choose protection for the exposure. Assess potential contact with blood, body fluids, mucosa, and nonintact skin. Add transmission-based precautions when indicated by local policy and current organism-specific guidance.
  3. Protect the next interaction. Perform hand hygiene at required moments; gloves do not replace it. Remove PPE safely and clean or appropriately reprocess shared equipment before the next patient.

Key point: Standard precautions are the starting point for every patient—not a label reserved for known infections.
