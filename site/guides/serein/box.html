Serein · Breathing · 4–12 cycles

Box Breathing

Follow a steady rhythm, one phase at a time. Holds are optional.

Follow the light around the square. Each side marks a phase. You can turn holds off and breathe continuously.

Steps

  1. Breathe in. Gently, and only as far as feels comfortable.
  2. Hold gently. Only if comfortable. No strain, no forcing.
  3. Breathe out. Gently, and only as far as feels comfortable.
  4. Rest gently. Only if comfortable. No strain, no forcing.

Safety: Never strain or force a breath hold. Choose “No holds” if holding is uncomfortable. If unsure about breath holds because of a health condition, seek advice from your clinician.

Serein offers short calming practices. It is not a treatment and does not replace professional care. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or your doctor.

Do it with a guide
