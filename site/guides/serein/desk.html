The Seated Reset

Ease your shoulders, uncurl your hands, and gently turn your head.

Sit on a stable, non-wheeled chair, with feet supported. Use a small comfortable range. The figure is a schematic cue, not a posture assessment.

Steps

  1. Shoulder lift & release. Gently lift your shoulders a little, then let them lower.
  2. Open & soften hands. Slowly uncurl your fingers, then let your hands soften.
  3. Gentle neck turns. Turn left within a comfortable range, return to center, then right.

Safety: Skip any movement that is unsuitable, painful, or makes you dizzy. Do not force a stretch or roll your neck. Check with a clinician if unsure whether these movements are suitable.

Serein offers short calming practices. It is not a treatment and does not replace professional care. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or your doctor.

Do it with a guide

Serein guides you through this practice with gentle timing and sound. Always free, for everyone who cares for others.
