Abu Dhabi DOH nursing exam: format, topics and how to prepare

The Abu Dhabi Department of Health Registered Nurse is set by the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi (DOH). It has 150 multiple-choice questions over 3 hours. Here is what the official sources say, where they disagree, and a 7-day plan.

A 2024 official DOH nursing licensing circular identifies Registered Nurse CBT as 150 MCQs over 3 hours. Domain weights and pass mark remain unverified; UAE PQR is a qualifications framework, not an exam blueprint.

Topics

The regulator has not published how many questions come from each topic. Study the main nursing areas evenly:

  • Nursing Fundamentals
  • Adult Nursing
  • Maternal–Child Nursing
  • Management and Professional Practice

How many attempts?

UAE-wide rules allow three attempts across authorities, with a possible final attempt subject to approval. Confirm with DOH.

A 7-day study plan

  1. Day 1: read the official guide and note the topics, time and rules.
  2. Day 2: do 30 mixed practice questions and list your weak topics.
  3. Day 3: study your two weakest topics, then answer 30 questions on them.
  4. Day 4: priority and safety questions: airway, breathing, circulation, then risk.
  5. Day 5: a timed block of questions at exam pace. Review every miss and why.
  6. Day 6: a full mock if one is available, or two timed blocks. Review again.
  7. Day 7: light review only. Check your ID, booking and test-centre rules. Sleep.

Rules change. Always check the regulator before you book.
