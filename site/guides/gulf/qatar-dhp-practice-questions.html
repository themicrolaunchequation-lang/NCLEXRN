10 free DHP practice questions

Question 1. In a Qatar ward, an RGN is asked to perform an unfamiliar elective procedure. The nurse has no assessed competence in it. What is the best response?

Answer: D. The request does not establish competence. Arranging qualified help protects the patient without abandoning care. Trap to avoid: Remote instructions do not establish assessed competence.

Question 2. A Qatar RGN plans to delegate a stable patient’s scheduled care task. What must be checked before choosing the team member?

Answer: B. Willingness and availability do not establish authority or ability. Delegation must fit the person and the task. Trap to avoid: Workload matters only after role and ability are established.

Question 3. In Qatar, an RGN has delegated care to a competent colleague. Which action best meets the RGN’s ongoing responsibility?

Answer: B. Delegation does not end the need for oversight. Outcome review helps identify care that needs adjustment. Trap to avoid: Waiting for a complaint misses earlier signs of incomplete care.

Question 4. Before an elective procedure in Qatar, a patient says, “I signed because I thought this was a blood test.” What should the nurse do first?

Answer: C. A signature alone does not show informed agreement. The misunderstanding must be addressed before the elective procedure proceeds. Trap to avoid: A form does not resolve the stated misunderstanding.

Question 5. A capable adult in Qatar wants the clinician to explain another treatment option. A relative asks the nurse to keep to the original plan. What is the best response?

Answer: A. Care should support the patient’s informed participation. Family involvement should not prevent the patient from receiving information. Trap to avoid: Family preference does not displace a capable adult’s voice.

Question 6. On a Qatar ward, a visitor asks for an adult patient’s laboratory results. The nurse has not established permission to share them. What is the best response?

Answer: A. The request alone does not authorize disclosure. Identity and a valid basis for access must both be established. Trap to avoid: Selected results are still confidential information.

Question 7. During a bedside discussion in Qatar, nearby visitors can hear sensitive details. The patient is stable. What should the nurse do?

Answer: B. Avoidable exposure of personal information should be reduced. Omitting a name does not make an identifiable bedside conversation private. Trap to avoid: Room context can still identify the patient.

Question 8. A stable patient in Qatar requests a same-gender practitioner for intimate care. What should the nurse do first?

Answer: D. Preferences should be discussed individually and respected where feasible. The nurse must also account for the urgency of care. Trap to avoid: An individual preference must not be generalized without discussion.

Question 9. After giving prescribed pain relief in Qatar, which entry best supports continuing care?

Answer: B. The record should show what was done and how the patient responded. Intended outcomes must not be recorded as observed facts. Trap to avoid: A broad completion statement omits important clinical details.

Question 10. A Qatar ward nurse hands over a patient with a new oxygen requirement. Which information is most important to communicate?

Answer: A. A new oxygen requirement is a relevant change in condition. The receiving team needs the current situation and outstanding actions. Trap to avoid: The chart does not replace communication of a significant change.

DHP exam questions people ask

How many questions are in the Qatar DHP nursing exam?

150 multiple-choice questions. The current DHP exam page says 3 hours; an older DHP blueprint document says 3½ hours, so check the time on your booking confirmation.

What is the DHP exam pass mark?

The published pass mark is 50%.

How many times can I take the DHP exam?

DHP policy (v04) and the qualifying-examination page describe five attempts. A three-attempt line sits under physician specialties and does not clearly apply to nurses. Effective dates are not stated; not confirmed in writing by DHP.

Are these real DHP exam questions?

No. Original practice material, not official exam questions. Not endorsed by SCFHS, DHP, DHA or DOH. They are written to match the exam's style and topics.
