5 free DOH practice questions

Question 1. A patient with chronic hypercapnic respiratory disease deteriorates and requires oxygen. Which approach is most appropriate?

Answer: A. Oxygen therapy should treat hypoxemia while being individualized and reassessed. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 2. After supporting a postoperative patient with acute dyspnea, what should the nurse do next?

Answer: A. After treatment, reassess from the beginning to confirm earlier priorities remain controlled. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 3. A patient with severe asthma has loud wheezing, then becomes quieter while remaining tachypneic and exhausted. What is the priority interpretation?

Answer: C. A quiet chest in severe asthma can represent critically reduced airflow rather than improvement. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 4. A septic patient is febrile and confused. Which finding requires the most immediate response?

Answer: B. Profound hypotension with poor perfusion is an immediate circulation threat. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 5. A patient suddenly becomes dyspneic after central-line removal. Which action should the nurse take first?

Answer: A. Sudden respiratory deterioration requires immediate oxygenation support and escalation while the cause is assessed. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

DOH exam questions people ask

How many questions are in the Abu Dhabi DOH nursing exam?

150 multiple-choice questions in 3 hours (DOH nursing licensing circular, 2024). DOH reports a pass or fail result and does not publish its pass mark.

What is the DOH exam pass mark?

DOH reports a pass or fail result and does not publish its pass mark.

How many times can I take the DOH exam?

UAE-wide rules allow three attempts across authorities, with a possible final attempt subject to approval. Confirm with DOH.

Are these real DOH exam questions?

No. Original practice material, not official exam questions. Not endorsed by SCFHS, DHP, DHA or DOH. They are written to match the exam's style and topics.
