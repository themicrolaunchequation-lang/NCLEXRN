Qatar DHP nursing exam: what to expect and how to prepare

The Qatar Registered General Nurse Qualifying Examination is set by the Department of Healthcare Professions (DHP), Ministry of Public Health. Here is the format, the topics and a 7-day plan, from the regulator's own documents.

By Healthcare Crown Academy · Last updated 30 October 2026

150Questions
3 hoursTime
50%Pass mark

Format

The current DHP exam page lists 3 hours. An older official blueprint says 3½ hours, so confirm the time when you book.

DHP publishes 50% as the minimum score.

Topics

  • Nursing Fundamentals30 questions
  • Adult Nursing61 questions
  • Maternal–Child Nursing44 questions
  • Unit Management and Leadership15 questions

Attempts

DHP documents describe up to five attempts for nurses. Confirm with DHP before you book.

A 7-day study plan

  1. Day 1 Read the official guide. Note the topics, the time and the rules.
  2. Day 2 Answer 30 mixed questions. List your weakest topics.
  3. Day 3 Study your two weakest topics, then answer 30 questions on them.
  4. Day 4 Priority and safety: airway, breathing, circulation, then risk.
  5. Day 5 Answer a timed block at exam pace. Review every miss and why.
  6. Day 6 Take a full mock, or two timed blocks, and review again.
  7. Day 7 Light review only. Check your ID, booking and test-centre rules, then sleep.

Rules change, so check the regulator before you book.
