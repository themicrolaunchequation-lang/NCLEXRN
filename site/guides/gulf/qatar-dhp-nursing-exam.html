Qatar DHP nursing exam: format, topics and how to prepare

The Qatar Registered General Nurse Qualifying Examination is set by the Department of Healthcare Professions (DHP), Ministry of Public Health. It has 150 multiple-choice questions over 3 hours. Here is what the official sources say, where they disagree, and a 7-day plan.

How many attempts?

DHP policy (v04) and the qualifying-examination page describe five attempts. A three-attempt line sits under physician specialties and does not clearly apply to nurses. Effective dates are not stated; not confirmed in writing by DHP.

A 7-day study plan

  1. Day 1: read the official guide and note the topics, time and rules.
  2. Day 2: do 30 mixed practice questions and list your weak topics.
  3. Day 3: study your two weakest topics, then answer 30 questions on them.
  4. Day 4: priority and safety questions: airway, breathing, circulation, then risk.
  5. Day 5: a timed block of questions at exam pace. Review every miss and why.
  6. Day 6: a full mock if one is available, or two timed blocks. Review again.
  7. Day 7: light review only. Check your ID, booking and test-centre rules. Sleep.
