Gulf Region nursing exams

The four Gulf nursing exams compared

Where you want to work decides which exam you sit: Saudi Arabia (SNLE), Qatar (DHP), Dubai (DHA) or Abu Dhabi (DOH). Dubai and Abu Dhabi are both in the UAE but use different regulators.

ExamRegulatorQuestionsTimePass standardTopic weights published?

Dubai DHA nursing examDubai Health Authority (DHA)1503 hours50% published threshold; reported as pass or failNo
Abu Dhabi DOH nursing examDepartment of Health – Abu Dhabi (DOH)1503 hoursNot verifiedNo

Figures come from each regulator's published documents, linked on each exam page. Where sources disagree we say so. Check with the regulator before you book.
