5 free SNLE practice questions

Question 1. A nurse receives handoff on four patients. Which patient should be assessed first?

Answer: C. New stridor after extubation suggests an evolving upper-airway obstruction. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 2. A patient with pneumonia has SpO2 84%, RR 34/min, and accessory-muscle use. What should the nurse do first?

Answer: C. The patient has an immediate breathing problem requiring support and reassessment. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 3. A patient with an upper GI bleed becomes pale and restless; BP falls to 82/48 mmHg and HR rises to 132/min. What should the nurse do first?

Answer: A. Hypotension, tachycardia, pallor, and restlessness with bleeding indicate circulatory instability. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 4. A patient suddenly becomes difficult to arouse. Airway is patent, SpO2 96%, RR 16/min, and BP 126/74 mmHg. What should the nurse do next?

Answer: D. With airway, breathing, and circulation adequate, the new neurologic change is the next priority. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

Question 5. A trauma patient has gurgling respirations and blood in the mouth. Which action is most appropriate?

Answer: A. Gurgling with oral blood indicates threatened airway patency. Trap to avoid: Do not use ABCDE Priority Framework as a rigid slogan; compare the patient's actual severity, timing, safety, scope, and response.

SNLE exam questions people ask

How many questions are in the Saudi SNLE nursing exam?

200 multiple-choice questions in two blocks of 100, each 120 minutes, with a scheduled break. SCFHS reports a scaled score from 200 to 800; 500 is the pass mark.

What is the SNLE exam pass mark?

SCFHS reports a scaled score from 200 to 800. The pass mark is 500.

How many times can I take the SNLE exam?

SCFHS sources differ: the applicant guide describes up to four attempts a year, while the SCFHS dates page describes three attempts in an initial cycle with renewal provisions after a year. This is not a lifetime maximum. Confirm with SCFHS before relying on either.

Are these real SNLE exam questions?
