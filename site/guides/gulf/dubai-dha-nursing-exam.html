Dubai DHA nursing exam: what to expect and how to prepare

The Dubai DHA Registered Nurse Assessment is set by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA). Here is the format, the topics and a 7-day plan, from the regulator's own documents.

150Questions
3 hoursTime
50%Pass mark

Format

The 3 hours includes registration and the introduction, so the question time is a little shorter.

DHA publishes 50% as the pass mark. Results are reported as pass or fail.

Topics

The regulator has not published how many questions come from each topic. Expect the main nursing areas:

  • Nursing Fundamentals
  • Adult Nursing
  • Maternal–Child Nursing
  • Unit Management and Leadership

Attempts

Across UAE health authorities you generally have three attempts. A fourth needs permission, and after a third failure there can be a wait before trying again. Confirm with DHA.

A 7-day study plan

  1. Day 1 Read the official guide. Note the topics, the time and the rules.
  2. Day 2 Answer 30 mixed questions. List your weakest topics.
  3. Day 3 Study your two weakest topics, then answer 30 questions on them.
  4. Day 4 Priority and safety: airway, breathing, circulation, then risk.
  5. Day 5 Answer a timed block at exam pace. Review every miss and why.
  6. Day 6 Take a full mock, or two timed blocks, and review again.
  7. Day 7 Light review only. Check your ID, booking and test-centre rules, then sleep.

Official sources

Rules change, so check the regulator before you book.
