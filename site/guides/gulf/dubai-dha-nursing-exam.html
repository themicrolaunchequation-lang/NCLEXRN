Dubai DHA nursing exam: format, topics and how to prepare

The Dubai DHA Registered Nurse Assessment is set by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA). It has 150 multiple-choice questions over 3 hours. Here is what the official sources say, where they disagree, and a 7-day plan.

DHA CBT guideline (Sep 2026) lists Registered Nurse as 150 questions, 3 hours, 50%. The 3 hours includes registration and introduction. Domain weights, breaks and block structure are not published in the sources reviewed.

Topics

The regulator has not published how many questions come from each topic. Study the main nursing areas evenly:

  • Nursing Fundamentals
  • Adult Nursing
  • Maternal–Child Nursing
  • Unit Management and Leadership

How many attempts?

General DHA licensing rules apply: three attempts across UAE authorities, a fourth only with permission, and a two-year block after the third failure before new attempts. Sources differ on details; confirm with DHA.

A 7-day study plan

  1. Day 1: read the official guide and note the topics, time and rules.
  2. Day 2: do 30 mixed practice questions and list your weak topics.
  3. Day 3: study your two weakest topics, then answer 30 questions on them.
  4. Day 4: priority and safety questions: airway, breathing, circulation, then risk.
  5. Day 5: a timed block of questions at exam pace. Review every miss and why.
  6. Day 6: a full mock if one is available, or two timed blocks. Review again.
  7. Day 7: light review only. Check your ID, booking and test-centre rules. Sleep.
