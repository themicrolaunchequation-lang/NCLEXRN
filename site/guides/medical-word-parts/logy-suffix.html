Medical suffix

-logy meaning

The medical suffix -logy means study or field; often taught with linking o as -ology. A common example is cardiology.

Words that use -logy

  • Ophthalmology: The specialty that looks after eye health.
  • Serology: Study or testing of serum, often for antibodies.
  • Andrology: Clinical field concerned with male reproductive health.
  • Sperm morphology: Assessment of sperm shape and structure.
  • Gynaecology: Specialty concerned with female reproductive health.
  • Cervical cytology: Microscopic examination of cells collected from the cervix.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -logy mean?

-logy means study or field; often taught with linking o as -ology. Example: cardiology.

What does -lysis mean?

-lysis means breakdown, separation or destruction. Example: hemolysis.

What does -lytic mean?

-lytic means pertaining to breakdown or destruction. Example: hemolytic.

What does -malacia mean?

-malacia means softening. Example: osteomalacia.

What does -megaly mean?

-megaly means enlargement. Example: hepatomegaly.
