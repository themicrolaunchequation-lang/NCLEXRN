Medical suffix

-ial meaning

The medical suffix -ial means pertaining to. A common example is bronchial.

Words that use -ial

  • Medial: Closer to the centre line.
  • Action potential: Rapid propagated change in a cell membrane's electrical potential.
  • Superficial: Closer to the body surface.
  • Antimicrobial: Agent active against microorganisms.
  • Randomised controlled trial: Experiment assigning interventions using a random process.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ial mean?

-ial means pertaining to. Example: bronchial.

What does -ian mean?

-ian means person associated with a field. Example: pediatrician.

What does -iasis mean?

-iasis means condition, often a pathological process. Example: psoriasis.

What does -iatric mean?

-iatric means pertaining to medical treatment or a specialty. Example: pediatric.

What does -iatrist mean?

-iatrist means medical practitioner or specialist. Example: psychiatrist.
