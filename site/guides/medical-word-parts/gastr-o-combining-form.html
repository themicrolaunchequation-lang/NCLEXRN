Medical combining form

gastr/o meaning

The medical combining form gastr/o means stomach. A common example is gastritis.

Words that use gastr/o

  • Gastritis: The lining inside the stomach is inflamed.
  • Gastroenteritis: The stomach and intestines are inflamed.
  • Gastroscopy: A flexible scope is used to look inside the upper digestive tract.
  • Gastro-oesophageal reflux disease: Reflux causing troublesome symptoms or complications.
  • Gastrostomy: Creation of an opening into the stomach.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
