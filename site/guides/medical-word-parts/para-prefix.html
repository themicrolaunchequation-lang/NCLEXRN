Medical prefix

para- meaning

The medical prefix para- means beside, abnormal or near, depending on context. A common example is parathyroid.

Words that use para-

  • Parathyroid gland: Gland producing parathyroid hormone.
  • Parathyroid hormone: Hormone increasing blood calcium through several mechanisms.
  • Parasympathetic nervous system: Autonomic division supporting restorative and visceral functions.
  • Paraesthesia: Abnormal sensation such as tingling.
  • Paraparesis: Weakness of both lower limbs.
  • Paraplegia: Paralysis affecting both lower limbs.
  • Paraphimosis: Retracted foreskin trapped behind the glans.
  • Parasite: Organism living in or on a host at the host's expense.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
