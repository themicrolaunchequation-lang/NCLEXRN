Medical suffix

-cytopenia meaning

The medical suffix -cytopenia means decreased cell number; compound ending. A common example is pancytopenia.

Words that use -cytopenia

  • Thrombocytopenia: There are fewer platelets than expected.
  • Pancytopenia: Reduction of red cells, white cells and platelets.
  • Lymphocytopenia: A reduced circulating lymphocyte count.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -cytopenia mean?

What does -cytosis mean?

-cytosis means increased cell number; compound ending. Example: leukocytosis.

What does -desis mean?

-desis means surgical binding or fusion. Example: arthrodesis.

What does -dynia mean?

-dynia means pain. Example: mastodynia.

What does -eal mean?

-eal means pertaining to. Example: peritoneal.
