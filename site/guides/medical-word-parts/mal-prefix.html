Medical prefix

mal- meaning

The medical prefix mal- means bad, poor or abnormal. A common example is malabsorption.

Words that use mal-

  • Malignant: Cancerous, with the potential to invade or spread.
  • Malabsorption: Impaired intestinal uptake of nutrients.
  • Malleus: Middle-ear ossicle attached to the tympanic membrane.
  • Male infertility: Impaired male reproductive capacity contributing to failure to conceive.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does meso- mean?

meso- means middle. Example: mesoderm.

What does meta- mean?

meta- means beyond, change or after. Example: metaplasia.
