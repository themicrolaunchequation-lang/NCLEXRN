Medical prefix

pre- meaning

The medical prefix pre- means before. A common example is prenatal.

Words that use pre-

  • Prenatal: Before a baby is born.
  • Prevalence: How common a condition is in a population.
  • Pressure injury: Local tissue damage associated with pressure or shear.
  • Precocious puberty: Development of pubertal changes earlier than expected.
  • Preload: Ventricular loading or stretch before contraction.
  • Premature beat: A cardiac beat occurring earlier than expected.
  • Presyncope: Symptoms suggesting impending fainting without loss of consciousness.
  • Presbyopia: Age-related reduction in near focusing ability.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

Five questions. Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does pre- mean?

pre- means before. Example: prenatal.

What does primi- mean?

primi- means first. Example: primigravida.

What does pro- mean?

pro- means before, forward or promoting. Example: proenzyme.

What does pros- mean?

pros- means towards or in addition. Example: prosencephalon.

What does pseudo- mean?

pseudo- means false or resembling. Example: pseudocyst.
