Medical prefix

meta- meaning

The medical prefix meta- means beyond, change or after. A common example is metaplasia.

Words that use meta-

  • Metastasis: Cancer has spread to another location.
  • Metaphysis: Region between a long bone shaft and end.
  • Metabolic acidosis: Primary acid-base disturbance involving reduced bicarbonate or increased acid.
  • Metaplasia: Replacement of one mature cell type by another.
  • Metabolism: Chemical modification of substances, including medicines.
  • Meta-analysis: Statistical combination of results from multiple studies.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
