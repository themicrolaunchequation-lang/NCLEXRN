Medical prefix

an- meaning

The medical prefix an- means absence or lack, before a vowel. A common example is anaesthesia.

Words that use an-

  • Angioplasty: A narrowed blood vessel is opened wider.
  • Anaemia: The blood has less haemoglobin than expected.
  • Anhedonia: Activities that usually feel enjoyable no longer do.
  • Anterior: Towards the front.
  • Ankylosis: Abnormal stiffness or fusion of a joint.
  • Anhidrosis: Absent or markedly reduced sweating.
  • Angio-oedema: Swelling in deeper skin or mucosal tissues.
  • Anisocytosis: Variation in red blood cell size.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does an- mean?

an- means absence or lack, before a vowel. Example: anaesthesia.

What does ana- mean?

ana- means up, back or again. Example: anabolism.

What does ante- mean?

ante- means before. Example: antenatal.

What does anti- mean?

anti- means against or opposing. Example: anticoagulant.

What does apo- mean?

apo- means away or separated from. Example: apocrine.
