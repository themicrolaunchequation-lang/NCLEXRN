Medical prefix

con- meaning

The medical prefix con- means together or with. A common example is congenital.

Words that use con-

  • Contracture: Persistent shortening limiting normal joint movement.
  • Contusion: Bruising from blunt injury.
  • Contact dermatitis: Skin inflammation caused by contact with an irritant or allergen.
  • Congenital adrenal hyperplasia: Inherited enzyme disorders affecting adrenal steroid synthesis.
  • Congenital heart disease: Structural cardiac abnormality present from birth.
  • Constipation: Difficult or infrequent passage of stool.
  • Concussion: Traumatic brain injury causing transient disturbance of brain function.
  • Conjunctiva: Membrane covering the anterior sclera and inner eyelids.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does con- mean?

con- means together or with. Example: congenital.

What does contra- mean?

contra- means against or opposite. Example: contralateral.

What does de- mean?

de- means removal or reversal. Example: dehydration.

What does deca- mean?

deca- means ten. Example: decapeptide.

What does di- mean?

di- means two or double. Example: diplegia.
