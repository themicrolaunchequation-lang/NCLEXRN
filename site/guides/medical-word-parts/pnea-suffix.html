Medical suffix

-pnea meaning

The medical suffix -pnea means breathing. A common example is apnea.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

Quick check

Five questions. Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does -pnea mean?

-pnea means breathing. Example: apnea.

What does -pnoea mean?

-pnoea means british spelling corresponding to -pnea. Example: dyspnoea.

What does -poiesis mean?

-poiesis means formation or production. Example: hematopoiesis.

What does -poietic mean?

-poietic means pertaining to formation or production. Example: hematopoietic.

What does -praxia mean?

-praxia means purposeful action or movement. Example: apraxia.
