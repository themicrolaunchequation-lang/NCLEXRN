Medical prefix

omni- meaning

The medical prefix omni- means all. A common example is omnivorous.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does omni- mean?

omni- means all. Example: omnivorous.

What does ortho- mean?

ortho- means straight, upright, correct or normal. Example: orthopnoea.

What does pan- mean?

pan- means all or entire. Example: pancytopenia.

What does para- mean?

para- means beside, abnormal or near, depending on context. Example: parathyroid.

What does penta- mean?

penta- means five. Example: pentadactyly.
