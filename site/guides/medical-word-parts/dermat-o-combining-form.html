Medical combining form

dermat/o meaning

The medical combining form dermat/o means skin. A common example is dermatology.

Words that use dermat/o

  • Dermatitis: The skin is inflamed.
  • Contact dermatitis: Skin inflammation caused by contact with an irritant or allergen.
  • Atopic dermatitis: Chronic itchy inflammatory skin disorder associated with atopy.
  • Dermatome: Skin region supplied mainly by one spinal nerve root.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
