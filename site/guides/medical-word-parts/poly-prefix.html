Medical prefix

poly- meaning

The medical prefix poly- means many or much. A common example is polyuria.

Words that use poly-

  • Polycythaemia: Increased red cell concentration or mass.
  • Polycythaemia vera: Myeloproliferative neoplasm causing increased red cell production.
  • Polydipsia: Excessive thirst.
  • Polyphagia: Excessive hunger or eating.
  • Polyuria: Excessive urine volume.
  • Polycystic kidney disease: Inherited disorders with multiple renal cysts.
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome: Endocrine syndrome involving ovulatory dysfunction and androgen-related features.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
