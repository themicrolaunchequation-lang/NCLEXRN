Medical prefix

re- meaning

The medical prefix re- means again or back. A common example is reabsorption.

Words that use re-

  • Reduction: Restoration of a fracture or dislocation to alignment.
  • Reticulocyte: An immature red blood cell.
  • Red cell distribution width: Measure of variation in red cell volume.
  • Reticulocyte count: Measurement of immature red blood cells.
  • Receptor: Molecule that recognises a specific signal.
  • Restrictive cardiomyopathy: Cardiomyopathy with impaired ventricular filling from stiff myocardium.
  • Residual volume: Air remaining after maximal expiration.
  • Respiratory rate: Number of breaths per minute.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does re- mean?

re- means again or back. Example: reabsorption.

What does retro- mean?

retro- means backward or behind. Example: retroperitoneal.

What does semi- mean?

semi- means half or partial. Example: semilunar.

What does sub- mean?

sub- means under or below. Example: subcutaneous.

What does super- mean?

super- means above or beyond. Example: superinfection.
