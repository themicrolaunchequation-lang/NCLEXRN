Medical suffix

-ary meaning

The medical suffix -ary means pertaining to. A common example is pulmonary.

Words that use -ary

  • Anterior pituitary: Front pituitary region producing several regulatory hormones.
  • Posterior pituitary: Pituitary region storing and releasing hypothalamic hormones.
  • Capillary: Microscopic vessel where exchange occurs with tissues.
  • Ovary: Gonad producing oocytes and reproductive hormones.
  • Pessary: Device placed in the vagina for support or treatment.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ary mean?

-ary means pertaining to. Example: pulmonary.

What does -ase mean?

-ase means enzyme. Example: lipase.

What does -blast mean?

-blast means formative cell; meaning depends on cell type. Example: osteoblast.

What does -capnia mean?

-capnia means carbon dioxide condition. Example: hypercapnia.

What does -cele mean?

-cele means herniation, swelling or protrusion. Example: cystocele.
