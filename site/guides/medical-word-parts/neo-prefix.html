Medical prefix

neo- meaning

The medical prefix neo- means new. A common example is neonatal.

Words that use neo-

  • Neonatal: Relating to the first weeks of a baby’s life.
  • Neoplasm: Abnormal new tissue growth.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does neo- mean?

neo- means new. Example: neonatal.

What does non- mean?

non- means not or absence of. Example: noninvasive.

What does nulli- mean?

nulli- means none. Example: nullipara.

What does octa- mean?

octa- means eight. Example: octapeptide.

What does oligo- mean?

oligo- means few or scanty. Example: oliguria.
