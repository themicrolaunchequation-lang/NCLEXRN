Medical suffix

-oid meaning

The medical suffix -oid means resembling. A common example is mucoid.

Words that use -oid

  • Keloid: Scar extending beyond the original wound margins.
  • Bullous pemphigoid: Autoimmune subepidermal blistering disease.
  • Adenoid: Lymphoid tissue in the nasopharynx.
  • Haemorrhoid: Symptomatic enlargement or displacement of anal vascular cushions.
  • Choroid: Vascular layer between sclera and retina.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -oid mean?

What does -ology mean?

-ology means study or field; extended form containing linking o. Example: dermatology.

What does -oma mean?

-oma means tumor or mass; does not establish malignancy. Example: lipoma.

What does -opia mean?

-opia means vision condition. Example: myopia.

What does -opsia mean?

-opsia means vision. Example: anopsia.
