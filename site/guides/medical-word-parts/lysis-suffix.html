Medical suffix

-lysis meaning

The medical suffix -lysis means breakdown, separation or destruction. A common example is hemolysis.

Words that use -lysis

  • Rhabdomyolysis: Breakdown of skeletal muscle releasing its contents.
  • Fibrinolysis: Breakdown of fibrin in a blood clot.
  • Haemolysis: Destruction of red cells with release of haemoglobin.
  • Glycogenolysis: Breakdown of glycogen.
  • Lipolysis: Breakdown of stored triglycerides.
  • Urinalysis: Physical, chemical or microscopic examination of urine.
  • Semen analysis: Laboratory assessment of semen and sperm characteristics.
  • Meta-analysis: Statistical combination of results from multiple studies.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -lysis mean?

What does -lytic mean?

-lytic means pertaining to breakdown or destruction. Example: hemolytic.

What does -malacia mean?

-malacia means softening. Example: osteomalacia.

What does -megaly mean?

-megaly means enlargement. Example: hepatomegaly.

What does -meter mean?

-meter means instrument for measurement. Example: spirometer.
