Medical combining form

cardi/o meaning

The medical combining form cardi/o means heart. A common example is cardiology.

Words that use cardi/o

  • Tachycardia: The heart is beating faster than expected.
  • Bradycardia: The heart is beating more slowly than expected.
  • Pericarditis: The protective sac around the heart is inflamed.
  • Myocarditis: The muscular tissue of the heart is inflamed.
  • Endocarditis: The inner surface of the heart is inflamed.
  • Electrocardiogram: A tracing of the heart’s electrical signals.
  • Myocardium: Muscular tissue of the heart.
  • Endocardium: Inner lining of the heart chambers and valves.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
