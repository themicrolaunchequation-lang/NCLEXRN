Medical prefix

semi- meaning

The medical prefix semi- means half or partial. A common example is semilunar.

Words that use semi-

  • Semicircular canal: Inner-ear structure detecting angular head movement.
  • Seminal vesicle: Gland contributing fluid to semen.
  • Seminiferous tubule: Testicular tubule where sperm develop.
  • Seminal: Relating to semen.
  • Seminoma: A type of testicular germ cell tumour.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does semi- mean?

semi- means half or partial. Example: semilunar.

What does sub- mean?

sub- means under or below. Example: subcutaneous.

What does super- mean?

super- means above or beyond. Example: superinfection.

What does supra- mean?

supra- means above. Example: suprapubic.

What does sym- mean?

sym- means together, an assimilated form of syn-. Example: symphysis.
