Medical prefix

di- meaning

The medical prefix di- means two or double. A common example is diplegia.

Words that use di-

  • Distal: Further from where a limb or structure begins.
  • Diaphysis: Shaft of a long bone.
  • Dislocation: Complete loss of normal joint alignment.
  • Discectomy: Surgical removal of intervertebral disc tissue.
  • Disseminated intravascular coagulation: Systemic clotting activation causing thrombosis and consumption of clotting components.
  • Differential count: Measurement of white blood cell subtypes.
  • Direct antiglobulin test: Test detecting antibodies or complement attached to red cells.
  • Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma: Aggressive lymphoma arising from large B lymphocytes.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does dia- mean?

dia- means through or across. Example: dialysis.

What does dis- mean?

dis- means apart, reversal or absence. Example: dislocation.

What does dys- mean?

dys- means difficult, abnormal or impaired. Example: dysphagia.

What does ec- mean?

ec- means out or outside. Example: ectopic.
