Medical prefix

ultra- meaning

The medical prefix ultra- means beyond. A common example is ultrasonography.

Words that use ultra-

  • Ultrasonography: Sound waves are used to create images inside the body.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does ultra- mean?

ultra- means beyond. Example: ultrasonography.

What does un- mean?

un- means not or reversal. Example: unconscious.

What does uni- mean?

uni- means one. Example: unilateral.

What does tri- mean?

tri- means three. Example: tricuspid.

What does trans- mean?

trans- means across or through. Example: transdermal.
