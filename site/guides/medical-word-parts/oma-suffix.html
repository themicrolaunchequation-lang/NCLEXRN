Medical suffix

-oma meaning

The medical suffix -oma means tumor or mass; does not establish malignancy. A common example is lipoma.

Words that use -oma

  • Basal cell carcinoma: A skin cancer arising from basal-type cells.
  • Squamous cell carcinoma: A malignant tumour of squamous epithelial cells.
  • Melanoma: A malignancy arising from melanocytes.
  • Lipoma: A benign tumour of fat tissue.
  • Multiple myeloma: Malignancy of plasma cells, usually involving bone marrow.
  • Lymphoma: Malignancy of lymphoid cells.
  • Hodgkin lymphoma: Lymphoma characterised by specific malignant cell and tissue features.
  • Non-Hodgkin lymphoma: A diverse group of lymphoid malignancies excluding Hodgkin lymphoma.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -opia mean?

-opia means vision condition. Example: myopia.

What does -opsia mean?

-opsia means vision. Example: anopsia.

What does -opsy mean?

-opsy means viewing or examination. Example: biopsy.

What does -osis mean?

-osis means condition or process; meaning varies. Example: fibrosis.
