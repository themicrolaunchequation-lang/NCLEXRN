Medical prefix

sym- meaning

The medical prefix sym- means together, an assimilated form of syn-. A common example is symphysis.

Words that use sym-

  • Sympathetic nervous system: Autonomic division supporting mobilisation and stress responses.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does sym- mean?

sym- means together, an assimilated form of syn-. Example: symphysis.

What does syn- mean?

syn- means together. Example: syndactyly.

What does tachy- mean?

tachy- means fast. Example: tachycardia.

What does tele- mean?

tele- means distant. Example: telemedicine.

What does tetra- mean?

tetra- means four. Example: tetraplegia.
