Medical suffix

-aemia meaning

The medical suffix -aemia means british spelling corresponding to -emia. A common example is anaemia.

Words that use -aemia

  • Hypoxaemia: There is too little oxygen in the arterial blood.
  • Anaemia: The blood has less haemoglobin than expected.
  • Hyperglycaemia: Blood glucose is higher than expected.
  • Hypoglycaemia: Blood glucose is lower than expected.
  • Uraemia: Waste products build up because the kidneys are not working adequately.
  • Bacteraemia: Bacteria are present in the blood.
  • Polycythaemia: Increased red cell concentration or mass.
  • Iron deficiency anaemia: Anaemia caused by insufficient available iron.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
