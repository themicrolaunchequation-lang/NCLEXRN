Medical prefix

bin- meaning

The medical prefix bin- means two, a form used before certain vowels. A common example is binocular.

Words that use bin-

  • Binocular vision: Coordinated use of both eyes.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does bin- mean?

bin- means two, a form used before certain vowels. Example: binocular.

What does brady- mean?

brady- means slow. Example: bradycardia.

What does cata- mean?

cata- means down or breaking down. Example: catabolism.

What does circum- mean?

circum- means around. Example: circumoral.

What does co- mean?

co- means together or with. Example: co-infection.
