Medical combining form

arthr/o meaning

The medical combining form arthr/o means joint. A common example is arthritis.

Words that use arthr/o

  • Arthralgia: A joint hurts.
  • Arthritis: One or more joints are inflamed.
  • Osteoarthritis: A joint condition affecting cartilage and surrounding joint structures.
  • Rheumatoid arthritis: An autoimmune inflammatory disease commonly affecting joints.
  • Arthroscopy: Inspection or treatment inside a joint using a scope.
  • Arthroplasty: Surgical reconstruction or replacement of a joint.
  • Arthrodesis: Surgical fusion of a joint.
  • Arthrocentesis: Needle aspiration of fluid from a joint.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
