Medical suffix

-ic meaning

The medical suffix -ic means pertaining to. A common example is gastric.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

Quick check

Five questions. Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does -ic mean?

-ic means pertaining to. Example: gastric.

What does -ician mean?

-ician means practitioner or person associated with a field. Example: physician.

What does -ics mean?

-ics means field of knowledge or practice. Example: orthopedics.

What does -ion mean?

-ion means process or action. Example: flexion.

What does -ism mean?

-ism means state or condition. Example: hyperthyroidism.
