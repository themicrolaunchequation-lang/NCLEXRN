Medical suffix

-capnia meaning

The medical suffix -capnia means carbon dioxide condition. A common example is hypercapnia.

Words that use -capnia

  • Hypercapnia: Raised carbon dioxide level in blood.
  • Hypocapnia: Reduced carbon dioxide level in blood.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -capnia mean?

-capnia means carbon dioxide condition. Example: hypercapnia.

What does -cele mean?

-cele means herniation, swelling or protrusion. Example: cystocele.

What does -centesis mean?

-centesis means surgical puncture, often to remove fluid. Example: thoracentesis.

What does -clasis mean?

-clasis means breaking, sometimes a deliberate surgical fracture. Example: osteoclasis.

What does -clast mean?

-clast means cell that breaks down or resorbs. Example: osteoclast.
