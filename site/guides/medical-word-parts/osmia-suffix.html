Medical suffix

-osmia meaning

The medical suffix -osmia means smell. A common example is anosmia.

Words that use -osmia

  • Anosmia: Loss of smell.
  • Hyposmia: Reduced sense of smell.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ostomy mean?

-ostomy means creation of a surgical opening. Example: colostomy.

What does -otomy mean?

-otomy means incision into. Example: tracheotomy.

What does -ous mean?

-ous means pertaining to. Example: cutaneous.

What does -oxemia mean?

-oxemia means oxygen condition in blood; compound ending. Example: hypoxemia.
