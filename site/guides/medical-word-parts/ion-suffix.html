Medical suffix

-ion meaning

The medical suffix -ion means process or action. A common example is flexion.

Words that use -ion

  • Hypertension: Blood pressure remains above the expected range.
  • Cognition: How a person thinks, understands and remembers.
  • Flexion: Movement decreasing the angle at a joint.
  • Extension: Movement increasing the angle at a joint.
  • Abduction: Movement away from the body midline.
  • Adduction: Movement towards the body midline.
  • Supination: Forearm rotation turning the palm anteriorly or upward.
  • Pronation: Forearm rotation turning the palm posteriorly or downward.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ion mean?

-ion means process or action. Example: flexion.

What does -ism mean?

-ism means state or condition. Example: hyperthyroidism.

What does -ist mean?

-ist means person who practises or specialises. Example: pharmacist.

What does -itis mean?

-itis means inflammation. Example: dermatitis.

What does -lith mean?

-lith means stone. Example: nephrolith.
