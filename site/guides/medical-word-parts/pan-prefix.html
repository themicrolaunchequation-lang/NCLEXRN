Medical prefix

pan- meaning

The medical prefix pan- means all or entire. A common example is pancytopenia.

Words that use pan-

  • Pancytopenia: Reduction of red cells, white cells and platelets.
  • Pancreatic islet: Hormone-secreting cell cluster within the pancreas.
  • Panhypopituitarism: Deficiency affecting multiple or all anterior pituitary hormones.
  • Pancreas: Organ producing digestive enzymes and hormones.
  • Pancreatitis: Inflammation of the pancreas.
  • Pancreatic insufficiency: Inadequate pancreatic function, commonly digestive enzyme secretion.
  • Panic attack: Abrupt episode of intense fear or discomfort with associated symptoms.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does pan- mean?

pan- means all or entire. Example: pancytopenia.

What does para- mean?

para- means beside, abnormal or near, depending on context. Example: parathyroid.

What does penta- mean?

penta- means five. Example: pentadactyly.

What does peri- mean?

peri- means around. Example: pericardium.

What does poly- mean?

poly- means many or much. Example: polyuria.
