Medical prefix

ir- meaning

The medical prefix ir- means not, an assimilated form of in-. A common example is irregular.

Words that use ir-

  • Iron deficiency anaemia: Anaemia caused by insufficient available iron.
  • Irritable bowel syndrome: Disorder of gut-brain interaction with abdominal pain and altered bowel habits.
  • Iris: Coloured structure controlling pupil size.
  • Iritis: Inflammation of the iris.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does ir- mean?

ir- means not, an assimilated form of in-. Example: irregular.

What does iso- mean?

iso- means equal or same. Example: isotonic.

What does juxta- mean?

juxta- means next to. Example: juxtaglomerular.

What does macro- mean?

macro- means large. Example: macrocytosis.

What does mal- mean?

mal- means bad, poor or abnormal. Example: malabsorption.
