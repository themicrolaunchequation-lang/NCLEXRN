Medical suffix

-clast meaning

The medical suffix -clast means cell that breaks down or resorbs. A common example is osteoclast.

Words that use -clast

  • Osteoclast: Cell that resorbs bone.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

Five questions. Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does -clast mean?

-clast means cell that breaks down or resorbs. Example: osteoclast.

What does -cyte mean?

-cyte means cell. Example: erythrocyte.

What does -cytopenia mean?

-cytopenia means decreased cell number; compound ending. Example: pancytopenia.

What does -cytosis mean?

-cytosis means increased cell number; compound ending. Example: leukocytosis.

What does -desis mean?

-desis means surgical binding or fusion. Example: arthrodesis.
