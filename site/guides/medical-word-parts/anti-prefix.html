Medical prefix

anti- meaning

The medical prefix anti- means against or opposing. A common example is anticoagulant.

Words that use anti-

  • Antigen: Substance recognised by an immune receptor.
  • Antibody: Immunoglobulin that binds a particular antigen.
  • Antigen presentation: Display of antigen fragments for recognition by lymphocytes.
  • Antidiuretic hormone: Hormone promoting renal water conservation, also called vasopressin.
  • Antimicrobial: Agent active against microorganisms.
  • Antibiotic: Medicine used to treat bacterial infections.
  • Antiviral: Agent acting against viral infection.
  • Antifungal: Agent acting against fungal infection.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

Quick check

Five questions. Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does anti- mean?

anti- means against or opposing. Example: anticoagulant.

What does apo- mean?

apo- means away or separated from. Example: apocrine.

What does auto- mean?

auto- means self. Example: autoimmunity.

What does bi- mean?

bi- means two. Example: bilateral.

What does bin- mean?

bin- means two, a form used before certain vowels. Example: binocular.
