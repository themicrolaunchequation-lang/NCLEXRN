Medical combining form

hemat/o meaning

The medical combining form hemat/o means blood. A common example is haematology.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does hemat/o mean?

hemat/o means blood. Example: haematology.

What does hemat/o · hem/o mean?

hemat/o · hem/o means blood. Example: haematology.

What does hepat/o mean?

hepat/o means liver. Example: hepatomegaly.

What does my/o mean?

my/o means muscle. Example: myopathy.

What does nephr/o mean?

nephr/o means kidney. Example: nephrology.
