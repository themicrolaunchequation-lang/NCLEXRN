Medical suffix

-lith meaning

The medical suffix -lith means stone. A common example is nephrolith.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

Quick check

Five questions. Tap an answer to see if you are right.

What does -lith mean?

-lith means stone. Example: nephrolith.

What does -lithiasis mean?

-lithiasis means stone formation or presence; compound ending. Example: nephrolithiasis.

What does -logist mean?

-logist means specialist in a field. Example: cardiologist.

What does -logy mean?

-logy means study or field; often taught with linking o as -ology. Example: cardiology.

What does -lysis mean?

-lysis means breakdown, separation or destruction. Example: hemolysis.
