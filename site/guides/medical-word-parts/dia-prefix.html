Medical prefix

dia- meaning

The medical prefix dia- means through or across. A common example is dialysis.

Words that use dia-

  • Diaphysis: Shaft of a long bone.
  • Diabetes mellitus: Metabolic disease characterised by persistent hyperglycaemia.
  • Diabetic ketoacidosis: Acute metabolic state with ketosis and acidosis related to insulin deficiency.
  • Diabetes insipidus: Disorder causing excessive dilute urine from impaired vasopressin action.
  • Diastole: Relaxation and filling phase of the cardiac cycle.
  • Diaphragm: Major muscle of inspiration separating chest and abdomen.
  • Diarrhoea: Passage of abnormally loose or watery stools.
  • Diabetic retinopathy: Retinal vascular disease associated with diabetes.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does dia- mean?

What does dis- mean?

dis- means apart, reversal or absence. Example: dislocation.

What does dys- mean?

dys- means difficult, abnormal or impaired. Example: dysphagia.

What does ec- mean?

ec- means out or outside. Example: ectopic.

What does ecto- mean?

ecto- means outside or outer. Example: ectoderm.
