Medical prefix

ec- meaning

The medical prefix ec- means out or outside. A common example is ectopic.

Words that use ec-

  • Ecchymosis: Larger area of bleeding into skin or mucosa.
  • Eczema: Inflammatory skin disorder commonly causing itch and dryness.
  • Echocardiography: Ultrasound assessment of cardiac structures and function.
  • Ectopic testis: Testis located outside its usual path of descent.
  • Ectocervix: Outer portion of the cervix projecting into the vagina.
  • Ectopic pregnancy: Pregnancy implanted outside the normal endometrial cavity.
  • Echogenicity: Ability of tissue to reflect ultrasound echoes.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does en- mean?

What does endo- mean?

endo- means within or inner. Example: endocardium.

What does epi- mean?

epi- means upon or above. Example: epidermis.
