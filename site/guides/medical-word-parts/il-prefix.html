Medical prefix

il- meaning

The medical prefix il- means not, an assimilated form of in-. A common example is illegible.

Words that use il-

  • Ileum: Final portion of the small intestine.
  • Ileus: Impaired intestinal propulsion without a mechanical blockage.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does il- mean?

il- means not, an assimilated form of in-. Example: illegible.

What does im- mean?

im- means not, an assimilated form of in-. Example: immobile.

What does in- mean?

in- means not; sometimes in or into, depending on context. Example: incontinence.

What does infra- mean?

infra- means below or beneath. Example: infraclavicular.

What does inter- mean?

inter- means between. Example: intercostal.
