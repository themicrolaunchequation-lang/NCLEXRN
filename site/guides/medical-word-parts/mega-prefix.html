Medical prefix

mega- meaning

The medical prefix mega- means large or enlarged. A common example is megacolon.

Words that use mega-

  • Megakaryocyte: Bone marrow cell that produces platelets.
  • Megaloblastic anaemia: Anaemia associated with impaired DNA synthesis and megaloblasts.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

mega- means large or enlarged. Example: megacolon.

What does mes- mean?

mes- means middle, a shortened form of meso-. Example: mesencephalon.

What does meso- mean?

meso- means middle. Example: mesoderm.

What does meta- mean?

meta- means beyond, change or after. Example: metaplasia.

What does micro- mean?

micro- means small. Example: microcephaly.
