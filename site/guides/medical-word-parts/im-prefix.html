Medical prefix

im- meaning

The medical prefix im- means not, an assimilated form of in-. A common example is immobile.

Words that use im-

  • Immunodeficiency: The immune system’s defences are reduced.
  • Impetigo: Contagious superficial bacterial skin infection.
  • Immunoglobulin: Protein family that includes antibodies.
  • Immunoglobulin G: Major antibody class in blood and extracellular fluid.
  • Immunoglobulin A: Antibody class prominent at mucosal surfaces.
  • Immunoglobulin M: Antibody class commonly prominent early in an immune response.
  • Immunoglobulin E: Antibody class associated with immediate allergy and parasite defence.
  • Immunoglobulin D: Antibody class found mainly as a B-cell surface receptor.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does im- mean?

im- means not, an assimilated form of in-. Example: immobile.

What does in- mean?

in- means not; sometimes in or into, depending on context. Example: incontinence.

What does infra- mean?

infra- means below or beneath. Example: infraclavicular.

What does inter- mean?

inter- means between. Example: intercostal.

What does intra- mean?

intra- means within. Example: intravenous.
