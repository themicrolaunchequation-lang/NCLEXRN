Medical suffix

-praxia meaning

The medical suffix -praxia means purposeful action or movement. A common example is apraxia.

Words that use -praxia

  • Apraxia: Impaired execution of learned actions despite adequate basic motor ability.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ptosis mean?

-ptosis means drooping or downward displacement. Example: blepharoptosis.

What does -rrhage mean?

-rrhage means bursting forth of blood; related form -rrhagia. Example: hemorrhage.

What does -rrhagia mean?

-rrhagia means excessive bleeding. Example: menorrhagia.

What does -rrhaphy mean?

-rrhaphy means surgical suturing. Example: herniorrhaphy.
