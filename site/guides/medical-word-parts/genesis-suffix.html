Medical suffix

-genesis meaning

The medical suffix -genesis means origin, formation or development. A common example is osteogenesis.

Words that use -genesis

  • Gluconeogenesis: Production of glucose from non-carbohydrate substrates.
  • Glycogenesis: Formation of glycogen from glucose.
  • Ketogenesis: Production of ketone bodies.
  • Spermatogenesis: Production of sperm cells.
  • Spermiogenesis: Transformation of spermatids into spermatozoa.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -genesis mean?

-genesis means origin, formation or development. Example: osteogenesis.

What does -genic mean?

-genic means producing, produced by, or arising in. Example: neurogenic.

What does -geusia mean?

-geusia means taste. Example: ageusia.

What does -gram mean?

-gram means record or image. Example: electrocardiogram.

What does -graph mean?

-graph means instrument for recording. Example: electrocardiograph.
