Medical suffix

-ac meaning

The medical suffix -ac means pertaining to. A common example is cardiac.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ac mean?

-ac means pertaining to. Example: cardiac.

What does -aemia mean?

-aemia means british spelling corresponding to -emia. Example: anaemia.

What does -al mean?

-al means pertaining to. Example: renal.

What does -algia mean?

-algia means pain. Example: neuralgia.

What does -ary mean?

-ary means pertaining to. Example: pulmonary.
