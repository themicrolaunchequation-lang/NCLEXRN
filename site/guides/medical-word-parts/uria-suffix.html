Medical suffix

-uria meaning

The medical suffix -uria means urine condition. A common example is polyuria.

Words that use -uria

  • Haematuria: Blood is present in urine.
  • Dysuria: Passing urine is painful or uncomfortable.
  • Oliguria: Less urine is being produced than expected.
  • Glycosuria: Glucose in the urine.
  • Ketonuria: Ketone bodies in the urine.
  • Albuminuria: Albumin in urine above the expected amount.
  • Proteinuria: Protein in urine above the expected amount.
  • Pyuria: White blood cells in urine.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -y mean?

-y means state, condition or process; context dependent. Example: atrophy.

What does -trophy mean?

-trophy means nourishment, growth or development. Example: hypertrophy.

What does -tripsy mean?

-tripsy means crushing. Example: lithotripsy.
