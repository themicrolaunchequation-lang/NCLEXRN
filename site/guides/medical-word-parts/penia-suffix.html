Medical suffix

-penia meaning

The medical suffix -penia means deficiency or reduction. A common example is leukopenia.

Words that use -penia

  • Thrombocytopenia: There are fewer platelets than expected.
  • Osteopenia: Bone mineral density lower than normal but above the osteoporosis threshold.
  • Pancytopenia: Reduction of red cells, white cells and platelets.
  • Leucopenia: A reduced white blood cell count.
  • Neutropenia: A reduced neutrophil count.
  • Lymphopenia: A reduced lymphocyte count.
  • Lymphocytopenia: A reduced circulating lymphocyte count.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

-pexy means surgical fixation. Example: orchiopexy.

What does -phagia mean?

-phagia means eating or swallowing. Example: dysphagia.

What does -phasia mean?

-phasia means speech or language; clinical definition matters. Example: aphasia.

What does -philia mean?

-philia means affinity or tendency; interpret the complete term. Example: eosinophilia.
