Medical combining form

aden/o meaning

The medical combining form aden/o means gland. A common example is adenoma.

Words that use aden/o

  • Lymphadenopathy: Lymph nodes are abnormal or enlarged.
  • Hidradenitis suppurativa: Chronic inflammatory follicular disease with painful nodules and tunnels.
  • Adenoid: Lymphoid tissue in the nasopharynx.
  • Lymphadenitis: Inflammation of lymph nodes.
  • Pituitary adenoma: Usually benign tumour of the anterior pituitary.
  • Adenomyosis: Endometrial glands and stroma within the uterine muscle.
  • Fibroadenoma: Benign breast tumour containing glandular and fibrous tissue.
  • Adenoma: Usually benign epithelial tumour with glandular differentiation.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
