Medical suffix

-graphy meaning

The medical suffix -graphy means process of recording or imaging. A common example is angiography.

Words that use -graphy

  • Radiography: Taking an X-ray image.
  • Tomography: Making images of thin sections through the body.
  • Ultrasonography: Sound waves are used to create images inside the body.
  • Echocardiography: Ultrasound assessment of cardiac structures and function.
  • Angiography: Imaging of blood vessels, often using contrast.
  • Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography: Endoscopic procedure accessing biliary or pancreatic ducts.
  • Electroencephalography: Recording of brain electrical activity.
  • Urography: Imaging of the urinary collecting system.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -graphy mean?

-graphy means process of recording or imaging. Example: angiography.

What does -ia mean?

-ia means condition or state. Example: bradycardia.

What does -ial mean?

-ial means pertaining to. Example: bronchial.

What does -ian mean?

-ian means person associated with a field. Example: pediatrician.

What does -iasis mean?

-iasis means condition, often a pathological process. Example: psoriasis.
