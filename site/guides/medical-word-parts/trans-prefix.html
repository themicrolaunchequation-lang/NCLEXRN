Medical prefix

trans- meaning

The medical prefix trans- means across or through. A common example is transdermal.

Words that use trans-

  • Transferrin: Protein transporting iron in blood.
  • Transfusion: Administration of blood or blood components.
  • Transient ischaemic attack: Transient focal neurological dysfunction from ischaemia without acute infarction.
  • Transurethral resection of the prostate: Removal of prostate tissue through a urethral instrument.
  • Transvaginal ultrasound: Pelvic ultrasound using a vaginal probe.
  • Transverse plane: Plane dividing the body into superior and inferior portions.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does ultra- mean?

ultra- means beyond. Example: ultrasonography.

What does un- mean?

un- means not or reversal. Example: unconscious.

What does uni- mean?

uni- means one. Example: unilateral.
