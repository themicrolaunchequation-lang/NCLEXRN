Medical prefix

hexa- meaning

The medical prefix hexa- means six. A common example is hexadactyly.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does hexa- mean?

hexa- means six. Example: hexadactyly.

What does holo- mean?

holo- means whole or entire. Example: holocrine.

What does homeo- mean?

homeo- means similar or stable. Example: homeostasis.

What does homo- mean?

homo- means same. Example: homozygous.

What does hyp- mean?

hyp- means below, a shortened form of hypo-. Example: hypoxia.
