Medical suffix

-algia meaning

The medical suffix -algia means pain. A common example is neuralgia.

Words that use -algia

  • Neuralgia: Pain follows a nerve pathway.
  • Arthralgia: A joint hurts.
  • Myalgia: Muscles feel painful.
  • Fibromyalgia: A condition involving widespread pain and associated symptoms.
  • Otalgia: Ear pain.
  • Mastalgia: Breast pain.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -ase mean?

-ase means enzyme. Example: lipase.

What does -blast mean?

-blast means formative cell; meaning depends on cell type. Example: osteoblast.

What does -capnia mean?

-capnia means carbon dioxide condition. Example: hypercapnia.
