Medical prefix

primi- meaning

The medical prefix primi- means first. A common example is primigravida.

Words that use primi-

  • Primigravida: This is their first pregnancy.
  • Primipara: A person who has had one pregnancy reach the defined parity threshold.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does primi- mean?

primi- means first. Example: primigravida.

What does pro- mean?

pro- means before, forward or promoting. Example: proenzyme.

What does pros- mean?

pros- means towards or in addition. Example: prosencephalon.

What does pseudo- mean?

pseudo- means false or resembling. Example: pseudocyst.

What does quadri- mean?

quadri- means four. Example: quadriplegia.
