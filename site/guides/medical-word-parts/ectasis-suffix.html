Medical suffix

-ectasis meaning

The medical suffix -ectasis means dilation or expansion. A common example is bronchiectasis.

Words that use -ectasis

  • Atelectasis: Loss of aeration with collapse of lung tissue.
  • Bronchiectasis: Permanent abnormal dilatation of bronchi.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

-emesis means vomiting. Example: hematemesis.

What does -emia mean?

-emia means blood condition. Example: hyperglycemia.

-esthesia means sensation. Example: paresthesia.
