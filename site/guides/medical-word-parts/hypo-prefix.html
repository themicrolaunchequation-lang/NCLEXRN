Medical prefix

hypo- meaning

The medical prefix hypo- means below normal or deficient. A common example is hypoglycaemia.

Words that use hypo-

  • Hypoxaemia: There is too little oxygen in the arterial blood.
  • Hypoxia: Body tissues do not receive or use enough oxygen.
  • Hypoglycaemia: Blood glucose is lower than expected.
  • Hypothyroidism: There is too little thyroid hormone activity.
  • Hypodermis: Subcutaneous tissue beneath the dermis.
  • Hypopigmentation: Reduced pigmentation of the skin.
  • Hypochromia: Reduced haemoglobin staining in red blood cells.
  • Hyposplenism: Reduced splenic function.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does hypo- mean?

hypo- means below normal or deficient. Example: hypoglycaemia.

What does il- mean?

il- means not, an assimilated form of in-. Example: illegible.

What does im- mean?

im- means not, an assimilated form of in-. Example: immobile.

What does in- mean?

in- means not; sometimes in or into, depending on context. Example: incontinence.

What does infra- mean?

infra- means below or beneath. Example: infraclavicular.
