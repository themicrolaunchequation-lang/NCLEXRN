Medical prefix

syn- meaning

The medical prefix syn- means together. A common example is syndactyly.

Words that use syn-

  • Synovium: Membrane lining a synovial joint.
  • Synovitis: Inflammation of a joint's synovial lining.
  • Syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis: Inappropriate water retention associated with impaired water excretion.
  • Syncope: Transient loss of consciousness due to cerebral hypoperfusion.
  • Synapse: Junction where a neuron communicates with another cell.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does syn- mean?

What does tachy- mean?

tachy- means fast. Example: tachycardia.

What does tele- mean?

tele- means distant. Example: telemedicine.

What does tetra- mean?

tetra- means four. Example: tetraplegia.

What does trans- mean?

trans- means across or through. Example: transdermal.
