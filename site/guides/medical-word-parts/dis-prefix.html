Medical prefix

dis- meaning

The medical prefix dis- means apart, reversal or absence. A common example is dislocation.

Words that use dis-

  • Distal: Further from where a limb or structure begins.
  • Dislocation: Complete loss of normal joint alignment.
  • Discectomy: Surgical removal of intervertebral disc tissue.
  • Disseminated intravascular coagulation: Systemic clotting activation causing thrombosis and consumption of clotting components.
  • Distal tubule: Nephron segment modifying electrolyte and fluid handling.
  • Distribution: Movement of a medicine between blood and body tissues.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does ec- mean?

ec- means out or outside. Example: ectopic.

What does ecto- mean?

ecto- means outside or outer. Example: ectoderm.

What does en- mean?

en- means in or within. Example: encysted.
