Medical combining form

ven/o · phleb/o meaning

The medical combining form ven/o · phleb/o means vein. A common example is venous · phlebitis.

Words that use ven/o · phleb/o

  • Ventricle: Lower pumping chamber of the heart.
  • Vena cava: Major vein returning systemic blood to the right atrium.
  • Venule: Small vein collecting blood from capillaries.
  • Ventricular tachycardia: Rapid rhythm arising from the ventricles.
  • Ventricular fibrillation: Disorganised ventricular electrical activity without effective pumping.
  • Venous insufficiency: Impaired venous return, often due to valve dysfunction.
  • Ventricular septal defect: Opening in the septum between the ventricles.
  • Ventilation: Movement of air into and out of the lungs.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.
