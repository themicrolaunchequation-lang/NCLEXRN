Medical prefix

meso- meaning

The medical prefix meso- means middle. A common example is mesoderm.

Words that use meso-

  • Mesothelioma: Malignancy arising from mesothelial lining cells.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does meta- mean?

meta- means beyond, change or after. Example: metaplasia.

What does micro- mean?

micro- means small. Example: microcephaly.

What does mid- mean?

mid- means middle. Example: midline.

What does mono- mean?

mono- means one or single. Example: monoplegia.
