Medical suffix

-pathy meaning

The medical suffix -pathy means disease or disorder. A common example is neuropathy.

Words that use -pathy

  • Neuropathy: One or more nerves are damaged or not working normally.
  • Lymphadenopathy: Lymph nodes are abnormal or enlarged.
  • Tendinopathy: A disorder of a tendon.
  • Radiculopathy: Dysfunction of a spinal nerve root.
  • Myopathy: A disorder primarily affecting muscle.
  • Coagulopathy: A disorder of blood clotting.
  • Monoclonal gammopathy: Presence of an immunoglobulin produced by one cell clone.
  • Cardiomyopathy: Disease affecting heart muscle structure or function.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -pathy mean?

-pathy means disease or disorder. Example: neuropathy.

What does -penia mean?

-penia means deficiency or reduction. Example: leukopenia.

What does -pexy mean?

-pexy means surgical fixation. Example: orchiopexy.

What does -phagia mean?

-phagia means eating or swallowing. Example: dysphagia.

What does -phasia mean?

-phasia means speech or language; clinical definition matters. Example: aphasia.
