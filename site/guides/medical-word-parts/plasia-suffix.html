Medical suffix

-plasia meaning

The medical suffix -plasia means formation or development. A common example is hyperplasia.

Words that use -plasia

  • Congenital adrenal hyperplasia: Inherited enzyme disorders affecting adrenal steroid synthesis.
  • Benign prostatic hyperplasia: Nonmalignant increase in prostatic tissue.
  • Cervical intraepithelial neoplasia: Precancerous epithelial changes in the cervix.
  • Dysplasia: Abnormal cellular growth and maturation.
  • Anaplasia: Marked loss of cellular differentiation.
  • Hyperplasia: Increase in the number of cells in a tissue.
  • Metaplasia: Replacement of one mature cell type by another.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does -plasty mean?

-plasty means surgical repair or reconstruction. Example: rhinoplasty.

What does -plegia mean?

-plegia means paralysis. Example: hemiplegia.

What does -pnea mean?

-pnea means breathing. Example: apnea.
