Medical prefix

ana- meaning

The medical prefix ana- means up, back or again. A common example is anabolism.

Words that use ana-

  • Anaemia: The blood has less haemoglobin than expected.
  • Anaphylaxis: A serious systemic hypersensitivity reaction.
  • Anal fissure: A linear tear in the anal canal lining.
  • Anaesthesia: Loss of sensation, sometimes deliberately induced.
  • Anatomical position: Reference posture standing upright with palms facing forwards.
  • Anaplasia: Marked loss of cellular differentiation.

How to decode a medical word

Split the word into its parts: a prefix at the front, a root in the middle and a suffix at the end. Read the suffix first, then the prefix, then the root. Learn each part once and you can read many new words.

What does ante- mean?

ante- means before. Example: antenatal.

What does anti- mean?

anti- means against or opposing. Example: anticoagulant.

What does apo- mean?

apo- means away or separated from. Example: apocrine.

What does auto- mean?

auto- means self. Example: autoimmunity.
