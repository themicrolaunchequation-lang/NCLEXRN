Control chart calculator (individuals and moving range)

Paste one value per time period, in time order, for example daily waiting times in minutes. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Reading the chart

  • Points outside the red limits point to a special cause worth investigating.
  • A run of 8 or more points on one side of the centre line suggests the process has shifted.
  • Do not change a stable process because of one bad day: that adds variation.
