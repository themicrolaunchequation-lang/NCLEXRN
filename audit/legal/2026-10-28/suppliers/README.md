# Supplier agreements: where to save them and what to save

**Best place:** a private Google Drive folder owned by the company account (themicrolaunchequation@gmail.com), named `HCA Legal / Supplier agreements / 2026`, shared with no one except your solicitor or accountant when needed. Keep a second copy on your computer. Do **not** put signed agreements or account screenshots in the code repositories (some are public or may become public).

Create one subfolder per supplier. In each, save:
1. The data processing agreement (DPA) **PDF** of the version in force (File → Print → Save as PDF if there is no download button). Put the date in the file name, e.g. `Supabase-DPA-2026-10-28.pdf`.
2. **Proof it applies to you:** a screenshot of your account's billing/organisation page showing the account name and plan, and (where offered) the accepted or signed DPA.
3. The supplier's **sub-processor list** (PDF).
4. One line in `EVIDENCE-LOG.csv` (template in this folder).

| Supplier | Where to get the DPA | Proof of acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Dashboard → your organisation → **Settings → Legal documents** (download the DPA there; Supabase also publishes it at supabase.com/legal/dpa) | Screenshot of the organisation page (name "clinicalai", plan) |
| Stripe | stripe.com/legal/dpa (part of the Stripe Services Agreement) | Screenshot of Stripe dashboard → Settings → Business details (legal entity and country) |
| Resend | resend.com/legal/dpa | Screenshot of Resend → Settings (team name) and Domains (region Ireland) |
| Vercel | vercel.com/legal/dpa | Screenshot of Vercel → Settings → Billing (team/plan) |
| Cloudflare | cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa | Screenshot of Cloudflare → Manage account → Billing (plan) |
| Netlify (only if still hosting the Green Belt site) | netlify.com, Legal → Data Processing Agreement | Screenshot of team billing page |

Then send me nothing: just tick each row in the log. If a supplier asks you to sign or click "accept" a DPA, do it from the company account.
