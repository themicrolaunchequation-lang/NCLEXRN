# A. Short prompt for the external solicitor (paste as is; attach LEGAL-REVIEW-PACK.pdf and CLASSIFICATION-BRIEF.pdf)

I run HEALTHCARE CROWN ACADEMY LTD, a UK company (England and Wales, 17501904, ICO ZC273259, not VAT-registered). Its registered office is a London registered-office service, but I run the business day to day from Saudi Arabia and Jordan. One director, no employees. We sell online learning to consumers worldwide through Stripe: a Crown Pass subscription (£5.99/month or £39.99/year, 7-day free trial), a one-off Gulf Pass (US$50/69, nursing exam practice), a one-off Lean Six Sigma Green Belt toolkit (US$99), and from June 2027 paid Evinca reviews. No real money has been taken yet. Please advise in plain English, with ready-to-use wording:

1. **Address:** which address must our website, checkout and emails show under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and the Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002, given that we are run from Saudi Arabia and Jordan? Is the registered office alone lawful? Must I show my home address, or what are the lawful alternatives?
2. **Service or digital content:** for each product, which is it, and what exact checkout wording do we need? Can we keep "full refund within 3 days, then a refund minus the time used, within 14 days", or must some products get a full refund within 14 days?
3. **Free trial:** does the 7-day trial change anything?
4. **Confirmation and cancellation emails:** do ours meet the law (samples attached)?
5. **The legal pack:** what still has to change before we take live payments? Give a must-fix list.
6. **Saudi Arabia and Jordan:** do their consumer, e-commerce, licensing or data-protection laws apply to us because we operate from there? What should a local lawyer check?
7. **Your fee and timescale** for items 1–5, and whether you can coordinate item 6 with a Saudi/Jordanian lawyer.

# B. Questions for a deep-search / research agent (facts to bring to the solicitor and accountant; not legal advice)

**UK consumer law**
1. Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, Schedule 2 and reg. 13, and Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002, reg. 6: what exactly is the "geographical address at which the trader is established"? Is there UK guidance or case law on a company whose registered office is a service address and which is run from abroad?
2. CCR regs 36 and 37: official guidance (BEIS/DBT, CMA, Which?, Citizens Advice) on whether online courses, question banks and subscription learning apps are "services" or "digital content", with examples.
3. What exact wording do the CMA and DBT give for the consent and acknowledgement needed to supply digital content early?
4. DMCCA 2024 subscription contracts regime: is the commencement date confirmed? What are the final rules for reminder notices, cooling-off on renewal, free trials and easy exit?

**Saudi Arabia**
5. Does the Saudi E-Commerce Law (2019) and its regulation apply to a foreign-registered company run by a resident of Saudi Arabia that sells online to consumers worldwide? What registrations (e.g. Maroof, Commercial Registration, MISA licence) are required?
6. Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL): does it apply to processing done from Saudi Arabia for worldwide users? What are the cross-border transfer rules, and are DPO or registration duties triggered?
7. Saudi VAT (ZATCA): rules for electronic services supplied to Saudi consumers by (a) a non-resident and (b) a business managed from Saudi Arabia. What are the thresholds?
8. Saudi zakat and income tax: does a UK company managed from Riyadh create a permanent establishment or tax residence in Saudi Arabia?

**Jordan**
9. Does Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law (2023) apply to processing done from Jordan? What are the transfer and registration rules?
10. Jordan general sales tax on digital services supplied by non-residents, and the rules if managed from Jordan.
11. Does managing a UK company from Jordan create Jordanian tax residence or a permanent establishment? What does the UK–Jordan tax treaty say?

**UK company and tax**
12. Is a UK-incorporated company always UK tax-resident? How do the UK–Saudi and UK–Jordan treaty tie-breakers (place of effective management) work for a one-director company?
13. Does Companies House or HMRC require anything when the director lives abroad, for example the service address and the director's residential address?

**Payments**
14. Does Stripe allow a UK Stripe account whose representative and operations are in Saudi Arabia or Jordan? Does Stripe's terms or KYC require operations in the UK?
15. Stripe Tax: does it support Saudi and Jordanian registrations? Which Gulf countries does it calculate tax for?
