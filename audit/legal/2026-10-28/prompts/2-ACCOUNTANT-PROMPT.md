# Prompt for an accountant (or a tax research agent whose answer an accountant will check)

---

Please give a **written, country-by-country conclusion** on indirect tax (VAT, GST, digital-services tax) for HEALTHCARE CROWN ACADEMY LTD before it accepts live sales.

**The company:** private limited company, England and Wales, 17501904, registered office at a London registered-office service. **One director, no employees. The business is run day to day from Saudi Arabia (Riyadh) and Jordan; nothing is done from the UK.** **Not UK VAT-registered.** No live sales yet; expected first-year turnover is small (owner to give an estimate). Stripe account is a UK account.

**What it sells, all online, automatically delivered, through Stripe Checkout:**
| Product | Price | Customers |
|---|---|---|
| Crown Pass subscription (online learning apps) | £5.99/month or £39.99/year; lower USD prices in some countries | consumers worldwide (UK, Gulf, EU, Asia, Africa) |
| Gulf Pass (exam practice for Saudi, Qatar, Dubai, Abu Dhabi nursing exams) | US$50 or US$69 one-off | mainly consumers in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE; also India, Philippines, Egypt and others |
| Lean Six Sigma Green Belt toolkit | US$99 one-off | consumers and some employers worldwide |
| Team plans by invoice | £20–£30 per learner, US$79–US$99 per seat | businesses (care homes, hospitals) |

Prices are shown as the total the customer pays. Stripe collects the customer's billing country.

**Questions:**
0. **Corporate tax and residence first.** The company is UK-incorporated but managed from Saudi Arabia and Jordan. Is it UK tax-resident (incorporation rule) and also potentially resident or a permanent establishment in Saudi Arabia or Jordan (place of effective management, director working there)? What filings follow in each country (UK corporation tax, Saudi zakat/income tax and CR/licensing, Jordan income tax), and how do the UK–Saudi and UK–Jordan tax treaties resolve dual residence or PE? Is a UK company still the right structure?
1. UK: when must the company register for VAT (threshold, how to monitor it), and how are these supplies treated (place of supply for B2C and B2B digital/e-learning services)?
2. EU: are these "electronically supplied services"? Is there any threshold before EU VAT is due for a UK seller (we understand there is none for non-EU sellers)? Should we register for the non-Union OSS from the first EU sale, or block EU sales until ready?
3. Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, and **Jordan (general sales tax on digital services)**: does a supplier of digital/e-learning services to consumers there have to register and charge VAT/GST, and from what threshold? Does the answer change for Saudi Arabia and Jordan because the business is run from there (resident vs non-resident rules)?
4. Other countries with likely sales (India, Philippines, Egypt, Nigeria, Pakistan, Kenya, Malaysia, Turkey, South Africa, Australia, Canada, US states): which have digital-services rules that would require registration from the first sale or a low threshold?
5. Can Stripe Tax handle this for us, and in which countries do we still need our own registrations?
6. How should prices be shown: is "the price shown is the total you pay, no VAT added" acceptable where tax applies, or must prices include local tax?
7. What records must we keep (customer location evidence, invoices) and for how long?

**Please answer as a table:** country · registration needed now? (yes/no/threshold) · rate · how to register/pay · deadline · action before first sale. Then a short list of what to do before switching on live payments, and whether to block any countries until registered.
